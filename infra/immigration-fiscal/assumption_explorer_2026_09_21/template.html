Fiscal assumption explorer

Start from one of these sets of assumptions

Each column runs the same ledger under a different convention, meaning a rule for what the account counts and how, and shows where the yearly result lands in billions of dollars. No commentator has produced a number for this population, so no column carries a person's name. What four of them argue is set out further down.

Assumptions

Each cost assumption is a share from 0 to 1: how much of the spending assigned to the group would not be needed without it. 1 charges the average cost per person. 0 treats the item as free to add people to.

A changed assumption shows a link back to its central value, and the tick under a slider marks that value. Point at anything to highlight what it touches.
