# Actual FY2024 custody payments: verified federal subtotal

**Verdict, September 20 follow-up:** Actual federal custody payments are publicly
reported. The initial acquisition's missing-spending statement was a source gap,
not evidence that spending cannot be measured. Two explicitly identified Custody
Operations rows report **$2,917,041,003 in FY2024 expenditures/outlays** and

## What remains to obtain for a complete total

The displayed ICE pages omit expired annual account 070 23 0540 and earlier
expired annual accounts. Payments from these need reconciliation against all
funding-vintage SF133 submissions and program-activity records (USAspending File B
or ICE's detailed execution records). Broad ICE or ERO totals are not custody totals.

Page 13 also reports fee accounts without custody splits: Immigration Inspection
User Fee 070 X 5382 and Breached Bond/Detention Fund 070 X 5126. Their whole-account
outlays cannot simply be added as detention costs. Construction, healthcare and
shared administration require an explicit boundary and overlap check.

A federal-plus-state/local account additionally needs actual local service costs
and matching federal receipts, with transfers canceled once. CBP short-term
custody and ORR care are separate programs if the requested boundary includes
them. This subtotal cannot be labeled all immigration enforcement spending or
attributed wholly to Mexican-origin people. [ACCOUNTING / MEASUREMENT]
