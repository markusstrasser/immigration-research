  CAVEAT: consecutive March ASEC samples share roughly half their households

  test above is therefore the conservative (easier to pass) direction.

PARAMETER NOTE: the 2026 run keeps the 2024 parameter set --- state sales tax rates,
effective property tax rates, ACS 2023 median gross rent, FY2024 per-pupil current
spending, ITEP quintile rates and the ACS 2024 pupil ratio --- exactly as the 2025
run uses them. Only the OASDI wage-base sensitivity updates ($168,600 -> $176,100,
IRS Publication 15 2024 and 2025). Nominal income grew between the two income years
while per-pupil cost and rent are held fixed, so the K-12 and renter items are
understated in real terms on the 2026 file; the columns are labelled accordingly.
