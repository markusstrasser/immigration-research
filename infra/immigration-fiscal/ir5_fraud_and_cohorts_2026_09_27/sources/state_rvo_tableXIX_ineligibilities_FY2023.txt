The ineligibilities overcome may not necessarily represent the same ineligibilities recorded in the total of ineligibility findings. Each action will be separately recorded as part of the appropriate statistical report for the year in which it occurred. A refusal can be overcome by evidence that the ineligibility does not apply, by approval of a waiver, or by other relief as provided by law.
