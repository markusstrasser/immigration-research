## Community vs university programs (primary studies)
- Kisielewski/APDIM-based study, "Where Do International Medical Graduates Matriculate for Internal Medicine Residency?"
  J Gen Intern Med (PMC7878164): "During that 12-year period [2007–2019], 55–70% of community programs' and 22–30% of
  university programs' PGY1 positions were filled by IMGs." "IMG PGY1s in community programs decreased from 70 to 55%
  during this time, whereas the percentage of IMGs in university programs decreased from 30 to 22%." Rank-list share
  to IMGs, 2017: university 15.7%, community 46.1%.
- "Separate but Equal? The Sorting of USMDs and Non-USMDs in Internal Medicine Residency Programs", J Gen Intern Med
  2020 (PMC7210370), FREIDA 2017–18, 476 programs: "76 (16%) IM programs were integrated, 119 (25%) were
  USMD-dominated, while another 201 (42%) were IMG-dominated, and 80 (17%) were DO-dominated." FREIDA did not separate
  U.S. IMGs from non-U.S. IMGs.
- "Reported Visa Acceptance or Sponsorship for Non-US Citizen Applicants to US Internal Medicine Residency Programs"
  (PMC9765918), FREIDA: 267 of 419 IM programs (63.7%) accept J-1; 92 sponsor H-1B and accept J-1. Authors: H-1B has
  "associated high administrative and legal costs, in contrast with J-1 visas for which residents assume the costs".
