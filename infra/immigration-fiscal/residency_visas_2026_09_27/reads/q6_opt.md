# Q6 reads: F-1 OPT counts and FICA estimates (fetched 2026-09-28 JST)

## ICE SEVP, 2024 SEVIS by the Numbers (published 2025-06-05)
https://www.ice.gov/doclib/sevis/btn/25_0605_2024-sevis-btn.pdf (via Exa; the guessed sevisBTN20xx.pdf URLs 404'd)
- "There were 194,554 pre- and post-completion optional practical training (OPT) students with both an employment
  authorization document (EAD) and who reported working for an employer in 2024, compared to 160,627 in 2023."
- "In 2024, a total of 194,554 foreign students obtained work authorization through OPT, while a separate population of
  95,384 foreign students obtained work authorization through STEM OPT, and 130,586 foreign students obtained work
  authorization through CPT." "the total number of unique foreign student SEVIS records with work authorization through
  a form of practical training was 381,140."
- STEM OPT participants: "165,524 foreign students participating in STEM OPT in 2024"; "India (48.0%) or China (20.4%)".
- SEVP data library, "2024 Total Number of SEVIS Records with authorizations to Participate in CPT, OPT or STEM-OPT":
  "CPT 140,775 OPT 340,066 STEM-OPT 165,524" (stock of records authorized at any time in 2024; OPT and STEM OPT overlap).
- SEVIS publishes no wages [GAP].

## Outside estimates (secondary; methods differ)
- Institute for Progress, "The Cost of the OPT FICA Exemption", 2026-01-22 (https://ifp.org/opt-fica-exemption/):
  "Each year from FY2017-2022, an average of 330,000 students participated in OPT. 85% of those OPT participants are
  exempt from FICA for at least some of the year." Closing the exemption (OPT Fair Tax Act, S.2940) "would increase
  federal revenue by $27–$36 billion over ten years, with a central estimate of $32 billion."
- Center for Immigration Studies (Feere), 2024-09-15: "$50,000 salary ... 15.3 percent, or $7,650. That amount times
  539,382 foreign students is over $4.1 billion" (539,382 = all practical training in 2023, including CPT).
  Quotes DHS's 2016 STEM OPT rule: "the employer saves an amount equal to 6.2 percent of the F-1 nonimmigrant's salary
  up to the taxable wage base ... and an additional 1.45 percent"; and DHS's reply to commenters that the revenue
  "does not approach the $4 billion over five years".
- Bills: S.2940 OPT Fair Tax Act (Sen. Cotton, Sept 2025, referred to Finance; Economic Times 2025-10-29); House bill
  of the same name by Rep. Grothman, 2026-05-21 (Fox News). Neither enacted as of the sources read.

## Employer preference
- Argued, not measured: Tax Notes, "Hiring Biases Fostered Under the Code" (2025-06-17) works a $100,000 example
  ($8,070 employer tax vs $0) and asserts employers "will always opt for the candidate who holds a student visa";
  IFP: "to the extent they can't [pass the cost to workers], the FICA exemption creates an incentive to hire foreign
  workers over Americans." No empirical study of hiring responses to the exemption was found [GAP].
