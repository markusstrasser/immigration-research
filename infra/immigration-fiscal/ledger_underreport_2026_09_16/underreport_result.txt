-- what is NOT corrected, and which way each omission cuts --
   1. The tax side is untouched. CPS ASEC FEDTAX_AC, STATETAX_A and FICA are Census
      MODEL output computed from reported income, not reported tax payments, so the
      survey-error literature on reported receipt does not apply to them. EITC and
      ACTC sit inside FEDTAX_AC and are likewise modeled, not reported.
   2. Because the arms add transfer dollars without re-running the tax model, they
      omit the tax that the added receipt would generate. UI is fully taxable and up
      to 85% of Social Security is taxable. Adding those dollars back would raise
      modeled tax more for the group receiving more of them, which is the white
      reference group. Correcting this would push the gap further NEGATIVE, i.e. the
      arms as run are biased toward the gap-narrowing direction.
   3. Earnings under-reporting is not addressed. w35680 covers transfer and pension
      receipt, not wage and salary income.
   4. Medicaid and housing assistance carry no dollar value in this ledger at all,
      so their (large) under-reporting cannot move it.
   5. Sampling error only. The SDR standard errors cover CPS sampling. They do not
      cover error in the published ratios, nor the transport of national ratios onto
      individual resource units.
