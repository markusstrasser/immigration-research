**Verdict:** The "white teens won't flip burgers, so McDonald's needs immigrants" picture is false in the data. US-born white teens have the highest employment rate of any group (37% of 16–19-year-olds, against 23% for US-born Black teens). Fast-food and counter workers are 51% US-born white and 9% foreign-born. US-born Black workers are 9.5% of fast-food and counter workers and 9.6% of all workers, so they are neither missing from these jobs nor over-represented in them. The survey records who holds a job, not how well the job is done.

Food service here is cooks, food preparation, fast food and counter, waiters, other servers, bussers, dishwashers and hosts: 6.93M workers (n = 2,887). Fast food and counter alone is 0.95M (n = 417), so its shares carry about ±3 points of sampling error. The survey counts people by their main job. Establishment surveys count jobs and report more fast-food workers. That changes the level, not the shares.

Immigrants are over-represented in back-of-house food jobs, mainly cooks and dishwashers: 24% of food-service workers against 20% of all workers. They are under-represented at the counter.
