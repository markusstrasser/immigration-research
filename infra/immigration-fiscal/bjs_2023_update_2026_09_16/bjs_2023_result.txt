Reading. The claim holds through the newest year (2022) on consistent Vintage-2024 denominators, and
holds for the whole 2021->2022 step on every top-level class. It FAILS at 2021 on the 'pep' basis for
violent only (white -23.5% vs Hispanic -21.6%), a sign flip caused entirely by the denominator fix:
the predecessor script divided 2021 white prisoners by the 2020 decennial count (191.7m) rather than
the July-2021 estimate (196.6m), which understated the white decline by about 2.5 points. On the
published 'orig' denominators the 2021 violent leg reads -20.5% white vs -22.5% Hispanic and the claim
holds, which is what ladder 70 and memo section 8 report. Adding 2022 restores the direction on both
bases, so the entry's conclusion survives; its violent margin at 2021 was inside denominator noise.

ICE-DETENTION INFLATION BOUND on the ACS foreign-born institutional count (ladder 65 caveat)
==============================================================================================================
ACS 2023 1-yr PUMS, men 18-39, foreign born, institutional group quarters (TYPEHUGQ=2): 65,685
ICE average daily detained population, FY2023: 28,289 (all ages, both sexes, all facility types)
Upper bound if EVERY ICE detainee were a foreign-born man 18-39 captured in ACS GQ: 43.1% of the cell
That bound is not attainable. ICE detention is roughly 85-90% male and a minority is outside 18-39;
ICE holds a large share of its population in local jails under IGSA contract, which the Census Bureau
enumerates as correctional GQ, and a further share in dedicated facilities. Applying 87% male and a
55-70% share aged 18-39 gives a plausible ICE contribution of
  13,536 - 17,228 detainees, i.e. 21-26% of the 65,685 foreign-born institutional cell.  [INFERENCE: the sex and age splits are assumptions]
Net of ICE, the foreign-born institutional rate for men 18-39 would be lower by that factor, which
widens rather than narrows the native-vs-foreign-born gap in the pro-immigrant direction.
Jail-side cross-check: BJS counts only 7,000 jail inmates held for ICE on the last
weekday of June 2023, 25% of ICE ADP, so most ICE detainees sit in
dedicated or contract detention facilities rather than in the local-jail universe BJS surveys.
