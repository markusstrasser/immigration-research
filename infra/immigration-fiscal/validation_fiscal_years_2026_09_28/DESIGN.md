# Frozen-profile temporal validation — fixed before scoring

2026-09-28. Retrospective holdout: these source years were previously inspected in other lanes. No claim of untouched prospective validation. Parent reviewed the design before scoring.

**Construct.** Can earlier CPS fiscal allocation patterns predict later observed CPS allocation shares, conditional on later population composition? This checks temporal transport of survey-derived component keys. It is neither a complete annual government account nor validation of service-response/no-immigration effects. Matching CPS also cannot validate tax imputation against actual collections.

**Population / units.** Civilian persons of every age (`PRPERTYP` 1/2). Disjoint observed groups: foreign-born Mexico-born G1; native-born with any Mexico-born parent G2; native-born with both US/territory-born parents and Mexican self-ID G3+; all others. The first three form the same observable union as the generation ledger. Ethnic attrition is not imputed. Interview-age, sex and current citizenship/birthplace define composition, whereas dollar fields refer to the prior calendar year.

**Read before design.** Existing base generator `build/analyze_cps_fiscal_2025.py`, ledger extension, matched-year tax lane, and official ZIP headers/codebooks. Use ZIPs for ASEC2022/23/24/25 (income2021/22/23/24), verify existing SHA256 pins. No download or raw mutation.

**Primary split:** income2022 → income2024. **Secondary:** income2022 → income2023. **Policy-regime stress:** income2021 → income2024, retained regardless of direction. No parameter tuning after seeing scores. Later-year outcomes never train profiles or choose cells. Four age bands [0,25), [25,45), [45,65), 65+ × two sexes × four groups =32 cells, fixed now. Each training cell must contain ≥20 sampled persons and positive full/replicate exposure; otherwise suppress the composition model for that split (no test-year filling). Export cell support.

**Outcomes.** Wages on own person with person weights; wages shared equally across SPM-unit members with unit-head weights; likewise federal income tax before and after refundable credits, CPS employee/self-employed payroll tax (`FICA`), SNAP, Social Security and SSI. The last seven use source totals allocated once, repeated SPM totals deduplicated; preserve both raw and weighted conservation. Keep personal wages separate from shared resources, and Census modeled taxes separate from observed earnings/reported receipts. Negative net income-tax cells remain legitimate. Fail on malformed tax identities, joins, missing values, weights or units.

**Prediction arms, same outcomes/score:**

1. Population proportional: later group exposure / later total exposure (in the estimator's own weight convention).
2. Frozen share: retain old group component share.
3. Group-size transport: old component per-person mean in each generation/rest group × later group exposure, normalized to total predicted component. This strips age/sex changes from arm4.
4. Age/sex/generation transport: old cell means × later cell exposures, normalized to total predicted component.

Each dollar error is share error × **observed later national CPS component total**. This is a known conditional scale, not a predicted or independently validated total. No inflation adjustment is needed for share scoring; common nominal scaling cancels. Policy changes by age/earnings (including 2021 expanded credits, SNAP pandemic allotments, payroll ceilings) need not cancel and are deliberately left to fail frozen profiles. This tests frozen profiles across those changes, not a tax-rule-aware microsimulation. No years are omitted for poor fit.

**Metrics / uncertainty.** Primary: absolute union-share error in percentage points per component; report signed errors and implied conditional dollar errors. Equal-component mean absolute share error is a convenience summary, with all component results retained and no adding wages to taxes or benefits. Secondary: total variation across all four disjoint group shares. Compare arm4 against frozen share with signed paired improvement in absolute error (positive = improvement), evaluated on the same held-out replicate. SDR coefficient4/160. Export held-out-sample and training-only SDR SEs separately; a conservative sum is a first-order bound allowing unknown cross-year covariance. No independent-years assumption, paired combined replicate assignment, or sampling-significance pass is claimed. No equivalence or accuracy threshold; the question is whether the richer rule beats the frozen-share baseline, with observed residual magnitude shown.

**External distribution diagnostic.** If published IRS income2022/23 table layouts and source hashes verify, transport the frozen2022 IRS income-tax shares over AGI bins to2023 and compare with the analogous frozen CPS return-based share. These independent administrative totals/distributions are diagnostic, not ethnic truth or additive fiscal corrections. Preserve statutory nominal AGI bins, call threshold migration part of the forecasting task, and do not tune on2023.

**Eligibility provenance / witness checks.** Source survey membership, group coding, age bands and estimator conventions determine inclusion independently of prediction arm. Toy old and later cells with identical outcome rates yield exact arm4 predictions (pass); reversing later age-specific rates with the same later counts gives a nonzero residual (fail). Population-only can win if group mean rates converge; frozen share can win if aggregate shares are stable despite composition change. Training support failure blocks arm4 rather than reading held-out outcomes. Missing or altered source hash blocks the run. No reward grader, paid work, adaptive stopping, or causal treatment-control experiment.

**Stop.** Fixed four survey years and three splits, plus the pinned two-year IRS diagnostic. All failed guards are reported and repaired before interpreting scores. A changed estimand would require an appended design revision before rerunning. No full-budget claim, confidence interval for structural model error, or national fiscal revision follows from this probe alone.

**Pre-score source-layout correction.** The first source guard stopped before aggregation: ASEC2022's `FEDTAX_AC` subtracts `CDC_CRD` and `EIP_CRD` as well as `ACTC_CRD` and `EIT_CRED`. The official ASEC2022 dictionary explicitly gives that identity. Later source headers omit the extra fields. Add the two fields only for survey2022 and retain their amounts in the planned net-tax stress outcome. This changes no split, population, score or fitted rule. A regression test rejects applying the later identity to the pandemic file.

The repaired guard then exposed ten survey2022 records with a one-dollar discrepancy from the published identity. Retain the released tax field; report the row count/raw residual, allow at most one dollar in that year, and reject larger discrepancies. Later years require exact identity. The source of this tiny residual is not identified here; its size is consistent with integer rounding but that explanation is not assumed as fact. No outcome score was available when this tolerance was set.
