The same-year CPS comparison is a measurement check, not a temporal forecast. [CALCULATION: `derived/irs_distribution.csv`]

The near-zero *ethnic* tax-share errors in section1 are consequently insufficient grounds for trusting tax allocation universally: a shared Census tax model and similar survey coverage can reproduce their own ethnic shares while continuing to allocate too little tax to high AGI bins. The result flags a distribution mismatch; it does not identify a proportional correction to Mexican-origin tax or justify adding the gap to the current account. ## 3. Nearby-year and pandemic-policy checks

Income2022→2023 gives mean absolute share error **0.423pp frozen →0.375pp composition transport**. Income2021→2024 gives **1.136pp→0.818pp**. These averages conceal the most consequential stress failure: transporting2021's after-refundable-credit tax profile to2024 underpredicts the target union's share by **4.769pp**, equivalent to **−$90.86bn** conditional on the2024 national CPS net-tax total. [CALCULATION]

That failure is consistent with the known policy incompatibility. The official ASEC2022 dictionary explicitly defines income2021's after-credit tax to subtract the expanded refundable child credit, child/dependent-care credit and Economic Impact Payment3, as well as EITC. The later files omit the extra CDC/EIP variables. The model intentionally holds old profiles fixed; it does not implement later tax law. Therefore the failure rejects transporting those unadjusted profiles across regimes, **not** tax-rule-aware forecasting in principle. Whether changes beyond the documented law account for the residual is not decomposed here. [SOURCE: [ASEC2022 dictionary](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cps/datasets/2022/march/asec2022_ddl_pub_full.pdf), `FEDTAX_AC` entry; CALCULATION / LIMIT]

Share scoring absorbs common nominal inflation but not policy changes with different effects across age, earnings or household composition. SNAP's pandemic allotment changes likewise remain possible profile-instability channels rather than reasons to drop unfavorable years. This lane does not estimate their separate contribution. [INFERENCE / LIMIT]

## 4. What this narrows, and what remains open

- **Retained:** raw earnings and component allocation keys have some useful temporal stability; adding demographic composition can improve prediction. Population-only allocation is a poor comparator for most selected components. [CALCULATION]
- **Qualified:** improving the mean score does not justify replacing every transfer key; all three selected transfer predictions worsen in the primary split. Close tax agreement within CPS cannot substitute for administrative distribution checks. [CALCULATION / INFERENCE]
- **Rejected for these data:** treating pandemic-era net-tax profiles as automatically transportable to2024. [CALCULATION]
- **Unresolved:** the true Mexican-origin tax distribution; how much survey errors persist in final calibrated keys; full historical government accounts; service responses; medical expenditure, institutions and general-equilibrium changes. Actual national totals were conditional inputs, so neither the magnitude nor sign of the complete present-day net cost is validated here. [LIMIT]

A future version should update legislated policy rules before testing residual transport and should use genuinely reserved administrative cells/years where available. Those are further models to test, not fixes inferred automatically from this exercise. [INFERENCE]
