# Annual account and counterfactual contract

Frozen before joining the new receipt, spending and benefit results, September20,2026.
Units are billions of2024 dollars per year. The target is the observed CPS
Mexican-origin union, all ages and schooling; beneficiaries in the welfare
calculation are other US residents. This is not a low-skill admission forecast.

1. Reconcile every2024 NIPA current receipt and expenditure once. Capital-account
   flows remain a separate accounting basis. Preserve national, target, other,
   external and genuinely unallocated amounts as distinct columns.
2. Complete modeled arms allocate every domestic pool using a named proxy;
   evidence-only arms retain unallocated amounts. Neither national reconciliation
   nor a proxy's institutional precedent validates its ethnic allocation.
3. Cross receipt and spending assumptions only within the same personal/shared
   convention. Report each assumption and diagnostic separately from the envelope.
4. The population-normalized comparison uses the same resident-assigned account
   on both sides: D = target balance - target population share * resident balance.
   Uniform per-person assignments cancel. Foreign and unallocated flows do not
   enter that resident balance. D is not a welfare term or a white benchmark.
5. Fiscal attribution and fiscal response are separate. A response coefficient
   means the fraction of a target-assigned flow assumed to disappear in the
   stationary absent-target economy. Historical debt interest and public assets
   have zero response in the primary conditional response arm. Public-service
   response and capital-related receipt overlap are explicit sensitivities.
6. The benefit bridge is W = P - M + beta*(A + F + M - O) + Z: P is outside
   after-tax private WTP; F is induced receipts; A is direct fiscal response;
   M is matched outside transfer savings; O is duplicated receipts; Z is omitted
   net external effects. beta=1 gives dollar-for-dollar fiscal recycling to the
   chosen beneficiaries; beta=0 is a separate valuation endpoint, not a bound.
7. Core capital ownership is all outside-resident; excluded-owner endpoints are
   separate. No price-surplus, crime or amenity dollars enter without matched
   beneficiaries and overlap. Report the Z or O required to reverse the sign.
8. The envelope is a declared model sensitivity, not a confidence interval or
   a proven bound. Preserve results that contradict the expected sign.

Response contract finalized before combining results: direct household and
personal-income taxes respond fully, but all corporate/property incidence,
other-business receipts and public assets respond zero. Household benefits
respond fully; business subsidies and legacy interest respond zero. Business
subsidies cannot be booked as a net cost to included owners without their private
receipt. Services respond0/.5/1 alongside capital adjustment0/.5/1 as an imposed
capacity path; the service-response break-even at each capital setting is also
exported. Defense/general-government response0/1 is independent. O=0 is the
favorable overlap endpoint; unresolved overlapping personal/capital-financed
taxes can only reduce that result. These are declared structural assumptions,
not estimated elasticities or policy time paths.
