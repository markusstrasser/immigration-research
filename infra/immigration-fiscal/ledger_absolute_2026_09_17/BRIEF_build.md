## Items and conventions
All charges are per CPS record in dollars for income year 2024; costs negative, receipts positive. Each item has one or more arms. Aggregate by group × age band with the full weight and each of the 160 replicates; SE = sqrt(4/160 Σ(rep−full)²). Also compute each item for the third-plus NH white reference and the all-native reference, and the common-age gap per standardized person (white-reference age shares, as `estimator.py` does), so the RESULT shows for every item: union total, per person, white per person, gap contribution.
