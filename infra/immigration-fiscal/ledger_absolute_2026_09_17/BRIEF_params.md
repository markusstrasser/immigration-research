2. **population**: NST-EST2024 resident population July 1, 2024 (US and by state). Keep the 2023 file too.
3. **deflator**: BEA NIPA price index for state and local government consumption expenditures and gross investment (Table 3.9.4 or 1.1.4 line for state and local), annual 2022 and 2024, to inflate the 2022 Census of Governments figures. Give both index values.
4. **enforcement**: DHS FY2024 enacted (from the FY2025 Budget in Brief or the FY2024 enacted tables): ICE total, ICE Enforcement and Removal Operations, ICE custody/detention operations, ICE Homeland Security Investigations; CBP total, US Border Patrol, Office of Field Operations; USCIS appropriated (non-fee) funds. DOJ EOIR FY2024 enacted. BOP FY2024 total and the share of BOP inmates who are Mexican nationals (BOP inmate statistics by citizenship, current snapshot).
5. **unauthorized**: DHS OHSS unauthorized immigrant population estimates, latest (January 2022 or newer): total and Mexico. Also the Pew or MPI latest if OHSS is older than 2022, labeled as secondary. Also DHS OHSS lawful permanent resident estimates, Mexico share, if on the same page.
6. **k12**: Census F-33 (Annual Survey of School System Finances) FY2023 or FY2024 state table: per-pupil current spending, total capital outlay, interest on school debt, fall membership, by state. Also the district-level F-33 file (elsec23t or 24) and the NCES Common Core of Data 2022–23 or 2023–24 district membership by race/ethnicity file (or the CCD "lea membership" flat file): download both, stage them, and give the file paths, the LEAID join key, and the column names for Hispanic, white, and total membership and for total current spending, capital outlay, and interest.
7. **ell**: California LCFF supplemental and concentration grant percentages (statute or CDE page); Texas bilingual education allotment weight (TEC §48.105); one national ELL cost-premium estimate from a published cost study with the range. ACS 2024 PUMS `ENG` variable codes (from the PUMS data dictionary) and `SCH`/`SCHG` codes for public school enrollment.
8. **state_medicaid_undocumented**: state general-fund cost of full-scope Medicaid-equivalent coverage for undocumented residents, FY2024 or FY2024–25 budget: California (LAO or DHCS estimate), Illinois (HBIA/HBIS), New York (Essential Plan expansion or state-only), Washington (Apple Health Expansion), Oregon (Healthier Oregon), Colorado, Minnesota, Massachusetts if any. Federal emergency Medicaid total (KFF or MACPAC, latest year).
9. **corporate**: JCT or Treasury OTA corporate tax incidence assumption (share to labor). Census Annual Survey of State Government Tax Collections 2024: corporate net income tax total, selective sales taxes total (motor fuel, alcohol, tobacco).
10. **underreporting**: program-level CPS ASEC dollar coverage ratios versus administrative totals, latest published (Census Bureau "ASEC vs administrative" or Rothbaum; Meyer, Mok and Sullivan 2015 as fallback) for SNAP, TANF, SSI, UI, housing assistance, Social Security, EITC. Also locate in this repo the lane that ran the "Meyer et al. w35680" under-reporting arms for the adult ledger (search `infra/immigration-fiscal` for `w35680` or `meyer`) and copy its parameter values with the file path.
11. **improper_payments**: paymentaccuracy.gov FY2024 (or FY2023) improper-payment dollars and rates for EITC, additional child tax credit, Medicaid, Medicare FFS, SNAP, SSI, UI.
12. **meps_coverage**: a published MEPS-to-NHEA reconciliation (AHRQ Statistical Brief or Bernard et al.) giving the MEPS share of NHEA personal health care spending, by payer if available (Medicaid, Medicare, private), and which NHEA components are excluded (nursing home, institutional, non-covered). NHEA 2023 totals: Medicaid, Medicare, personal health care, nursing care facilities.
13. **veterans_federal_retirement**: covered by omb (700) and the OMB civilian retirement subfunction (602); also the CPS ASEC variable names for veteran status and for federal pension income if distinct from other pensions (check `cpsmar25` documentation; report what exists).
