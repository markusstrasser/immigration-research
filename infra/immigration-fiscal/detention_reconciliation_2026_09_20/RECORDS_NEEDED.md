# Records required to complete the FY2024 detention account

Prepared specification only; no external request submitted and no fees authorized.
Seek existing electronic records, not an agency-created estimate. FY2024 means
payments October1,2023–September30,2024, regardless of appropriation vintage or
service date. Preserve service dates separately for cash/accrual reconciliation.

## ICE/DHS fiscal ledger

- Custody Operations PPA-II cash execution covering every open or expired funding
  year, including annual and multiyear0540, fee accounts5126/5382/5542, construction
  or procurement0545 where relevant, and reimbursable activity.
- Treasury account, fiscal period, appropriation year, program/subprogram/cost
  center, object class, gross disbursements, refunds, offsetting collections,
  reimbursements and net outlays. Include account/program code dictionaries and
  crosswalks between old/new or closed cost centers.
- The FY2024 Immigration User Fee Study and allocation schedules distinguishing
  custody, removal, investigations, legal and support activities; equivalent
  allocation records for other fee accounts. Preserve funding transfers separately
  from final service payments to prevent duplicate counting.
- Reconciliation to the September2024 monthly execution report and SF133, including
  prior-year corrections, shared support, medical costs for CBP-held people, and
  interagency bills. Describe which costs sit outside Custody Operations.

## Complete provider and payment universe

- Every facility used at any time in FY2024, including closed/zero-year-end sites,
  government and private operators, ICE use of USMS agreements, contract/IGSA
  identifiers, payee/vendor IDs and legal operator identities.
- Paid invoices and disbursement extracts: facility, agreement, payee, payer,
  service month, payment date, ICE civil detainee-days, guaranteed minimums,
  fixed/variable/per-diem components, separately paid medical/transport costs,
  refunds and adjustments. Seek aggregate fiscal records without detainee PII.
- Government pass-through agreements and subcontract/payment schedules distinguishing
  county service provision from transfers to private operators and administrative fees.

## Matching state/local records

For each public provider or pass-through entity, obtain matched-period general
ledger/cost-center transactions, ICE receipts and receivables, service payments,
personnel/benefit/medical costs, capital/debt schedules and custody-authority
activity splits. Reconcile county, sheriff and component-unit transactions once.
Keep other-custody revenue/expense separate. Document shared-cost allocation
methods; do not call an allocation a measured marginal policy cost.

If exhaustive records cannot be obtained, a national estimate requires a defined
provider universe and defensible sampling/allocation assumptions with uncertainty.
It must be labeled estimated. A request for exact records does not guarantee their
release, completeness or a common cost-accounting method across jurisdictions.
