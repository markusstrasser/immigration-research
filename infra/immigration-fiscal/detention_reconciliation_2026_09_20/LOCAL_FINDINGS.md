**Verdict:** A complete national state/local net cost of ICE civil detention in federal FY2024 is not identified by the public datasets examined. This is a concrete schema, coverage and accounting problem: the national finance files report broad government functions; ICE reports custody/facilities and contracts; neither supplies the paired ICE-purpose local expenditure and actual reimbursement ledger needed to close the account. No defensible national local-cost subtotal or tight numeric bound was recovered.

## What the national datasets actually contain

**Census2024 Annual Survey of Local Government Finances:** newly published July2026, with individual-unit ZIP revised August4. Official description specifies government unit IDs, item codes and amounts. The2024 methods document says all state governments plus11,443 sampled non-school local units (and additional education data), drawn from a universe exceeding90,000 units. Census item codes distinguish current operations for correctional institutions (`E04`) and other corrections (`E05`), capital outlay classes and broad federal intergovernmental revenue such as`B89` (“All Other”). They contain no ICE civil-custody-purpose allocation or facility-specific matched reimbursement field. Moreover, survey2024 covers local fiscal years ending July1,2023–June30,2024; this is not federal October2023–September2024. [Dataset/ZIP](https://www.census.gov/data/datasets/2024/econ/local/public-use-datasets.html), [2024 methods pp3–5](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances/tables/2024/2024_methodology.pdf), [item-code documentation pp24–25](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances/technical-documentation/complete-technical-documentation/estimatetechdoc.pdf).

**BJS Justice Expenditure and Employment Tool:** expenditure data are derived from Census finance collections, not an independent detention-purpose ledger. Variables are justice functions (corrections/police/judicial), levels of government, direct/current/capital expenditure, employment and payroll. JEET does not display intergovernmental expenditure separately. BJS corrections definitions combine institutional custody and noninstitutional correctional activities; temporary lockups under48hours belong to police protection. This cannot supply the missing ICE versus USMS versus local-criminal cost split. Sample design was not targeted specifically at justice spending. [BJS methods and definitions](https://bjs.ojp.gov/jeet-resources).

**ICE public FY2024 detention workbook already held:** facility sheet has names/location/agreement type, population and inspection information but no paid-dollar fields. Its exclusions include facilities with zero population at extraction and selected types. Thus it is not by itself the all-year vendor/facility universe. BJS jail-authority counts measure a midyear stock, not annual billable ICE-days. Local inspected evidence: `infra/immigration-fiscal/detention_evidence_2026_09_20/RESULT.md`, facility audit paragraph. Neither count×general-jail-cost nor contractual per-diem×stock yields actual local cost or receipts.

**IGSA documents:** the government named on a contract is not necessarily the jail operator. GAO documents IGSA arrangements in which the city passes federal payments to a private operator and receives a separate administrative fee. In its2021 investigation, ICE did not centrally track those privately negotiated fees; at least31 of1082019 IGSA facilities had private operators, but reliable full identification was unavailable. This is historical direct evidence of the missing accounting field, not proof that the same counts or tracking defect persist in2024. Current2025 GAO inspection work coversFY2022–24 facilities but does not publish a reconciled local-cost inventory. [GAO21-149 pp16–17](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-21-149.pdf), [GAO25-107580](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-25-107580/index.html).

## What the audited county example proves

**Baker County, Florida, year ended September30,2024:** note8 (printedp27, PDFp29) says its corrections management component unit reimbursed the Sheriff approximately$12.4m for actual correctional operations, including personnel, insurance and medical costs. This is **component-unit→Sheriff reimbursement**, not an identified federal ICE payment or ICE-only spending. The component unit's statement of activities (printedp10, PDFp12) reports total expenses$14,649,132 and service revenue$14,170,190, a$478,942 operating shortfall; including investment earnings gives a$455,063 decline in net position. These are facility/component-wide accrual figures and do not allocate ICE custody separately. Nor do they make the$12.4m internal reimbursement an additional cost to add again. [Audited source](https://flauditor.gov/pages/county_efile%20rpts/2024%20baker%20county.pdf).

The county's fiscal-year match is useful, but a custody-purpose split is still necessary. The audit cannot establish either an ICE-only loss or ICE-only zero cost. Berks' enterprise closure in2023, supplied as background, was not independently rechecked in this bounded investigation; no2024 subtotal is derived from it.

## Accounting and bounds

[ACCOUNTING IDENTITY] Let`F`be actual federal ICE civil-detention outlays, including payments to states/counties. Let`E_i`be state/local external expenditure allocated to that same custody purpose, and`R_i`all corresponding receipts financed directly or indirectly by the already-counted federal flow. Consolidated taxpayer expenditure is`F + sum(E_i - R_i)`, with internal state/local flows consolidated once and dates/recognition aligned. If a county receives100 and pays100 to operate or subcontract custody, its additional consolidated contribution is0; countingF plus100 local spending doubles the same financed service. Operator fees, grants, nonfederal revenues and timing adjustments need explicit placement rather than omission.

[CAUSAL DISTINCTION] Strictly incremental local fiscal impact is`delta E_i - delta R_i` relative to a no-ICE-contract counterfactual. Audited attributed costs alone cannot determine avoided fixed costs, displaced county prisoners, capacity expansion, or alternative facility use. A matched accounting balance and a marginal policy cost are distinct estimands.

[FRAMING] Taxpayer expenditure remains a cost even when detention is lawful/justified. Resource cost is a different concept: labor, buildings and supplies used have opportunity costs; an intergovernmental transfer is not another use of resources. Contract profit, debt principal, interest, depreciation and capital purchase cannot all be added as if they were separate contemporaneous resource consumption.

No positive observed local subset is automatically a national lower bound: unobserved jurisdictions can earn net surpluses. Zero is not a defensible lower bound on local net expense. All-corrections totals are not a useful hard upper bound without matching periods, exhaustive scope and nonnegative attribution assumptions; excluded police/public-works/legal/capital expenses also matter. Therefore no numerical national local add-on is supplied here.

## Exact missing records and completion route

The missing join is **complete FY2024 facility/vendor universe → legal contract/payee/operator IDs → actual invoice/service-month/paid-receipt records → local ICE-purpose operating/capital/overhead accounts**, covering sites that closed midyear and ICE use of USMS agreements. For each public operator, separate ICE civil detainee-days from USMS/local criminal custody and reconcile payer receipts to general-ledger accounts. For private pass-through IGSAs, obtain subcontract disbursements and administrative-fee receipts before assigning a public operating cost. Reconcile component units and federal/local timing; allocate shared costs explicitly and label marginal assumptions.

A targeted records request could seek ICE's completeFY2024 facility/payment ledger, paid IGSA invoices, vendor identifiers, service dates, separately billed medical/transport payments and contract/subcontract schedules; matching county cost-center records would still be needed. Published contracts alone cannot replace paid ledgers. FOIA or state public-records submissions/contact were **not** initiated; they require authorization. A national project would need verified population coverage or a declared probability sample with uncertainty, not opportunistic county anecdotes.
