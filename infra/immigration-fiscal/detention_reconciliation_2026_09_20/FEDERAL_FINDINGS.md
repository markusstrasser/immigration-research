## Gross/net audit follow-up

Treasury's [FY2024 Combined Statement, DHS chapter](https://fiscal.treasury.gov/system/files/files/reports-statements/combined-statement/cs2024/c18.pdf), printedpp11–12, explicitly reports **net** outlays. That field measures spending authority, not necessarily the cash offsets needed for this reconciliation. No equality or causal explanation is assumed merely from its name.

File B signed paid-prior-year refund fields USSGL487200 and497200 total -$11,407,457.32 for0540.
