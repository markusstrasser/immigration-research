**Verdict:** The City Administrative Officer's fee study gives the only primary, year-by-year administrative record found of how many City of Los Angeles sidewalk-vending permits were actually bought (about 944 a year against 16,000 planned) and what the program costs the General Fund; strong as an administrative count, silent on restaurants. Grade: B (administrative record; counts inferred from receipts for single years).
