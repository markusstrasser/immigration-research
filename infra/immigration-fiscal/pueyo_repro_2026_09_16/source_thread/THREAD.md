## 2099900552175882246 (likes 462)
When you get deep into the data, you realize a few things that resolve most debates:
1. In fact, in a lot of countries, overall crime rates are down! But it's because the native population gets older (so less crime), compensated by the influx of immigrants, who commit more crime

## 2099900555019665772 (likes 538)
2. Immigrants don't commit more of all crimes. They specialize in sex crimes &amp; violent assaults

But these are rare crimes compared to, say, pickpocketing

The result is that total crime stats (made disproportionately of petty crimes) can seem stable, while violent crime grows https://t.co/lQGjiKvvO8
- chart: charts/2099900555019665772_0.jpg (https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HSRV-dGW8AAJwp5.jpg)
