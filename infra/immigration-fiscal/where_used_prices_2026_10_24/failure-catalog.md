# Lane failures and regression guards

- **WP01 — Existing price counted twice.** The brief treats the K-12 1.034 factor and college E&R where-enrolled key as potential new increments. Traced primary callers show both are in oct16. Guard: `case_prices.cjs` checks the preferred school variant, fee bridge's 1.0341917412164, and reconstructed college key; both incremental operating entries are zero. Regression: `test_existing_k12_factor_is_not_added_again`, `test_primary_college_key_and_function_bridge_match_case`.
- **WP02 — School response derivative mislabeled as a school price.** Toggling `spec.school` changes the entire folded education line, including college and tuition terms. This lane never uses that derivative as a price base. The existing K-12 factor is checked at its source declaration. Regression: WP01's zero incremental control and `test_case_prices_use_full_evaluator_and_preserve_scope`.
- **WP03 — High FTE coverage hides unallocated capital.** System offices without assigned students hold material assets. Guard: print and retain finance-dollar coverage, excluded units and nonnegative allocation bounds, and label capital unidentified. `net_plant` has negative asset values and is screened out. Regression: `test_finance_coverage_and_unknown_assets_are_not_hidden_by_fte_coverage`.
- **WP04 — A city assigned to its headquarters county.** New York City's UID locates it in Manhattan, while its spending serves the city. Guard: join PID state+place exactly, reject duplicate places, leave consolidated-city mismatches unpriced. Regression: `test_new_york_uses_city_boundary_not_headquarters_county`, `test_unmatched_parks_are_bounded_not_silently_zero`.
- **WP05 — Municipal factor applied to the full parks/culture/capital budget.** Guard: replace only each state's matched E61 share inside the case's E61+F61+E52+F52 state-price budget; keep other governments, libraries, federal services and capital. Stock and acreage cannot substitute for a capital value. Regression: `test_park_state_price_principal_and_partial_budget_weights`, `test_tpl_is_a_geographic_sample_and_never_a_capital_price`.
- **WP06 — Missing applicable finance silently becomes zero.** Guard: only the primary `A` (not applicable) flag permits zero; a missing applicable value stops. Regression: `test_missing_applicable_finance_is_blocked_but_not_applicable_zero_is_explicit`.
- **WP07 — Raw-source serialization and zero cells.** Recreating the ACS ratios at full precision differed from the adopted six-decimal CSV ratio by 1.84e-8 in the college key. Preserve unrounded ratios, reproduce that explicit serialization for the principal check. Vermont has zero group annual and fall FTE in both degree cells; zero-to-zero raking leaves the cell at zero, while positive-to-zero stops. Regression: principal key and raking closure tests. Numerical values are converted to float before multiplying nullable string-derived Boolean masks (Arrow has no int64-times-Boolean kernel).
- **WP08 — External volume disappears mid-run.** `/Volumes/2TBPNY` unmounted after successful primary reconstruction. Guard: one explicit source resolver, recover only declared public inputs into `_cache/source_mirror/`, verify identical pinned SHA256, log every mirrored input, preserve original raw paths and write a recovery receipt. A different release stops; no summary substitutes for raw data. Regression: `test_all_raw_inputs_are_available_and_pinned_including_recovery_mirrors`, `test_source_mirror_rejects_corrupt_release_without_overwriting_raw`.
- **WP09 — Census response is HTML or errors expose credentials.** Guard: add the key internally on Census requests, suppress credential-bearing errors, validate JSON schema and B03001 fields. Codebook is acquired with the data; primary download manifests pin bytes and URLs with no key. The first keyless codebook request failed; the existing primary codebook was reused and its acquisition route repaired.
- **WP10 — Wrong state-price arm.** The state-price table has multiple weighting arms. Guard: require FY2024/direct/group/parks_libraries and a unique record before comparing the reconstructed 1.074678653 index. Regression: `test_park_state_price_principal_and_partial_budget_weights`.
