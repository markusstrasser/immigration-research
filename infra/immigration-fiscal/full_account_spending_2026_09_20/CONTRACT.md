# Spending assumptions declared before combined results

**Verdict:** This is an exhaustive conditional allocation of BEA calendar-2024 current expenditure, with a separate represented-only coverage diagnostic. It is not a measured ethnic administrative account or marginal immigration effect.

National partition: consumption by function from NIPA3.17 lines2–10; domestic benefit programs from3.12; explicitly foreign benefits/transfers and domestic/foreign interest from3.1; subsidies from3.13. All government grants between levels are consolidated by BEA. Consumption is net of sales and includes depreciation, but no gross investment/capital transfers are added. Published rounding is explicit. Amounts must sum to $10,061.458bn.

The complete arm allocates every domestic category using a named proxy. Target is the canonical all-age Mexican-origin union; other residents include other CPS civilians plus a separately reported outside-CPS domestic population allocation. Explicit BEA foreign/territory benefits, other foreign transfers and foreign interest remain external. Unexplained discrepancies never become institutional spending by relabeling. The outside-CPS domestic share is only an equal-cost-per-person scope assumption using the Census2024 resident population minus CPS2025 civilian population; it is not an observed program expenditure split. Target and other household shares use matching proxy totals conditional on the CPS civilian universe. Population and recipient denominators are exported.

Predetermined categories and alternatives:

- Cash social insurance/assistance: matching CPS dollar-weighted recipient share; personal versus equal SPM-unit sharing. Alternatives use age65+ for retirement, working ages for unemployment/workers compensation, and cash-assistance or adult population for residual assistance. These are mechanisms for conditional allocation, not measured nonresponse corrections.
- Medicare/Medicaid: separate raw MEPS2024 payer means transported by canonical age × US/not-US birthplace and reference-year exposure. Alternate age65+ / reported Medicaid coverage keys. No institutional add-on and no national2023-gap ethnic rake. Military/VA medical and other health services use corresponding public-payer or veteran proxies; alternatives use population. End-year-age exclusions in donors remain disclosed.
- Education services: the newly measured operating-school incidence key plus the existing postsecondary support key, excluding K/D capital/interest adjustments. Alternatives are operating-school-only and ages5–24. Education social benefits use postsecondary-support/age18–24 keys. NIPA's consumption function does not split K–12 and higher education here, so the mixture is explicitly a proxy, not an observed BEA suballocation.
- Refundable credits: CPS EITC+ACTC dollars versus adult-population proxy. BEA's program is broader; the CPS key does not observe every refundable credit. Full-pool transport is a declared assumption. Represented-only includes no more than observed CPS dollars and leaves the remainder unallocated.
- Housing subsidies: SPM housing-support key versus consumption/wage alternatives. Other subsidies: positive household resources/consumption versus labor income. They are benefit/cost allocation conventions, not established producer-to-household incidence.
- General public services, defense and domestic interest: `F_per_capita_average_cost` versus `F_fixed_target_zero`. In the latter, all target-attributed dollars move to other residents, never disappear. This changes accounting attribution only; fiscal response coefficients are a separate parent-owned analysis. Other public services use population or income/consumption keys.
- Represented-only diagnostic: categories with comparable observed dollars allocate up to those survey totals (and no more than the domestic household pool); residuals stay `unallocated_bn`. Consumption/public goods and indirect proxies have no directly matched dollar coverage and remain unallocated in this diagnostic. This is a coverage diagnostic, not a causal lower bound.

Endpoints are the listed source-based proxy/scope alternatives, not probabilities, empirical confidence bounds or a selected most-likely distribution. No signed balance or revenue is used to choose them. Sampling errors are not claimed for the aggregate incidence envelope; model/scope uncertainty dominates and parent owns cross-lane synthesis.
