## Disconfirmation note

Two angles above cut *against* the essay's thesis and should be kept: the AfD low-exposure/high-support pattern (immigration opposition is not driven by local contact, which weakens the "people can see it" premise) and the elderly-fiscal-burden comparison (a −$8.3k/adult-year gap is smaller than the intergenerational transfer the same budget runs to natives over 65). Ladder 80's "incumbent-student congestion is a precise zero in the best study" is the standing disconfirmation for the school-composition angle, and the PISA gap above is a *descriptive* composition fact, not a causal peer effect. Do not let one be read as the other.
