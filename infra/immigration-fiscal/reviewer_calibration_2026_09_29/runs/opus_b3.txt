CASE KEAC5
VERDICT: ERROR
LOCATION: The claim that the 24,000 count "is an upper bound", and the claim that noncitizen voting is orders of magnitude too small, set against margins of 6 and 187 votes
WHY: The audits leave out many states and cannot flag anyone never recorded as a noncitizen, so the true number could be higher. False positives push the other way, so the net direction is unknown and 24,000 is not an upper bound. Even taken at face value, 24,000 votes spread across 435 House districts averages about 55 per district, which is more than Iowa-2's 6-vote margin and within one order of magnitude of CA-13's 187, so ruling out any decided contest would need district-level counts.
CONFIDENCE: 0.9

CASE K4F6A
VERDICT: SOUND
LOCATION: none
WHY: Booking, in the exposure year, the present value of the lifetime loss that year causes is standard incidence-based accrual, like a pension's normal cost. Each year's exposure is counted once, 13 years of harm is 13 one-year accruals, and the tax loss appears only inside the social loss. The stated conditions are the ones that prevent a stock/flow error or double counting. This holds if each year's effect is estimated as the effect of one extra year, which the evidence implies.
CONFIDENCE: 0.65
