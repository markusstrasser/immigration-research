CASE KB713
VERDICT: ERROR
LOCATION: Comparing the 2009 and 2021 estimates across the methodology change.
WHY: The endpoints use different methods for assigning Hispanic origin. Revising the same 2018 population reduced its estimated count by about 28%, so the reported 26% decline cannot establish a real decline or its ranking against other groups.
CONFIDENCE: 0.99

CASE K4AF8
VERDICT: ERROR
LOCATION: “Did not lose ground” and “none of those mechanisms moves with the shares.”
WHY: The immigrant-origin estimate has an approximate 95% interval of −0.070 to +0.032 SD, allowing meaningful losses; statistical nonsignificance does not establish their absence. Likewise, cut-score intervals permitting declines of 7–13 points do not establish that standards are unrelated to the shares.
CONFIDENCE: 0.91

CASE KC896
VERDICT: ERROR
LOCATION: Treating 24,000 flags as a national upper bound and excluding every contest.
WHY: Incomplete audits with unmeasured missed cases cannot supply an upper bound, even when many flags are false positives. A national aggregate also cannot rule out effects in individual contests decided by 187 or 6 votes without geographically specific evidence.
CONFIDENCE: 0.99

CASE K6003
VERDICT: ERROR
LOCATION: Attributing the spike definitively to allocation and dismissing successive-cohort deterioration.
WHY: Redistributing allocated birthplaces in respondents’ proportions assumes that respondents represent those with missing birthplace; the sensitivity calculation does not establish that assumption. A later survey does not track the same individual men, and the matched-duration ratios actually increase across successive cohorts, 0.53→0.58→0.61, although their statistical significance is unknown.
CONFIDENCE: 0.91

CASE K0AD2
VERDICT: ERROR
LOCATION: Adding murder costs to assault and robbery costs that already include resulting deaths.
WHY: This counts homicide losses twice. Removing just the identified overlapping components subtracts $314 per adult-year, reducing the total to $1,107 and raising murder’s share to approximately 83.5%, rather than 65%.
CONFIDENCE: 0.99
