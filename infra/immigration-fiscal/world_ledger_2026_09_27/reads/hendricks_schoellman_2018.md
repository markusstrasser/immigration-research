**Verdict:** For Mexico, Hendricks and Schoellman measure an hourly wage gain at migration of 2.0 in the Mexican
Migration Project (pre $2.96, post $6.04, 2003 US dollars) and 1.8 in the NIS bin whose most sampled countries are
Mexico, Poland and Russia. Mexican immigrants are "roughly unselected" on pre-migration wages, have 7.1 years of schooling and are
over-represented in agriculture. The MMP's wages are the last jobs at home and in the US of household heads and
spouses interviewed in Mexico.

Used for: arm 5 of `beside_extra.py` (the same person's pay ratio) and its tenure check.
