# Lane: how many unauthorized residents are there, really — triangulating the stock by definition, coverage and flow (2026-09-19)

## Goal
The repo uses the unauthorized population inside the CPS ledger (ladder 85: Borjas-style residual on ASEC 2025 gives 14.9M nationally and 4.57M Mexico-born, against Pew 14.0M for July 2023 and DHS 11.0M for January 2022) and notes the CPS under-samples 2022–24 arrivals (3.9M vs CBO's 8.7M net other-foreign-national inflow). The operator's question: is the true number materially higher than the 14–15M the repo carries, and could any defensible definition or coverage assumption take it far above that (a figure of 40M was floated; test it explicitly, and state which population, if any, it corresponds to — the 40.9M Mexican-origin union across generations is mostly citizens and is not it). Produce a range by definition and by coverage assumption, with each arm's arithmetic exposed.

## Arms
1. **Published stock estimates, side by side, on one definition table.** DHS OHSS (January 2022 and any later release), Pew Research (2023 and any 2024/2025 update), Center for Migration Studies, Migration Policy Institute, Center for Immigration Studies (their January 2025 figure), CBO's demographic outlook. For each: reference date, definition (no lawful status only; or including parolees, TPS, DACA, pending asylum and other quasi-legal categories), data source (ACS or CPS), coverage adjustment applied, and Mexico-born share. Fetch the primary documents, never a news summary, and quote the definition sentence.
2. **Reproduce the residual on repo microdata.** ACS 2024 1-year PUMS (on disk, `~/research-data/immigration-fiscal/data/external/acs_pums_2024_1yr/`) and CPS ASEC 2025 (`cps/`), with the status-imputation rules already in `infra/immigration-fiscal/status_impute_2026_09_16/` (read-only; reuse, do not rewrite). Report the residual before and after the standard coverage multiplier, by region of birth and years since arrival.
3. **Coverage.** The Census 2020 Post-Enumeration Survey (Hispanic net undercount, published figure), Demographic Analysis, and the foreign-born coverage literature (Van Hook and colleagues on unauthorized coverage in the ACS; the Census Bureau's own foreign-born coverage studies). State the coverage multipliers each published estimate uses, and compute the range of the residual under multipliers from 1.0 to the highest published.
4. **Flow accounting 2021–2025.** CBP southwest-border encounters by disposition (Title 42 expulsions, releases with notices to appear, parole under CHNV and CBP One, transfers to ICE), the "gotaway" estimates DHS and the House Judiciary Committee have published, ICE removals and returns, USCIS parole and TPS grants, and the DHS 2025 removal and self-departure figures. Build a stock-flow identity from the January 2022 DHS stock forward: does the implied 2025 stock reconcile with the survey residuals, and by how much does the answer depend on the share of encounters that became residents rather than repeat crossers or removals? Compare with CBO's net-immigration series for 2021–2024 and its 2025 revision.
5. **Independent administrative anchors.** SSA Earnings Suspense File wage items and dollars (SSA OIG and Actuary publications); IRS ITIN filer counts; Mexican consular matrícula issuance; emergency Medicaid enrolment where states publish it; NYC, Chicago, Denver and Massachusetts shelter intake totals 2022–2025; K-12 enrolment of recent arrivals where published. Each is a partial signal with a known relation to the stock; use them to bound, not to point-estimate, and say what each can and cannot say.
6. **The 40M test.** Show the largest population any definition can produce: all non-citizens in ACS 2024 (about 24M before coverage), all foreign-born without naturalisation plus a maximal coverage multiplier, and the quasi-legal expansion. State plainly what number a maximal reading gives and why it is still below 40M, or if it is not, show the arithmetic.

## Outputs
`derived/`: estimate table with definitions and sources; residual reproduction table; coverage-multiplier grid; stock-flow reconciliation; anchors table; the 40M arithmetic. `RESULT.md` opening `**Verdict:**` with a central range for "no lawful status" and a separate range for "no permanent status including quasi-legal", each with the coverage assumption stated, and a one-paragraph answer to "is 14–15M too low".

## Limits to state
Residual estimates depend on legal-immigrant stock estimates that themselves carry error; coverage multipliers are assumptions; encounters are events, not people; quasi-legal categories are a definition choice and the memo must report both.
