# Research frontier: stronger questions across domains of life

**Verdict:** The largest remaining gap is between verified descriptions of groups and effects of specified policies on people's lives. Better measurement remains valuable, but neither more downloaded files nor more adjustments to the same sample can bridge that gap by themselves. The next substantial advance should close selected causal chains, measure who gains and loses, and make the values behind overall judgments explicit. This is a frontier assessment and recommendation, not an adopted change to the project's constitution, causal tree or analysis protocol. [INFERENCE from the inspected evidence below.]

Scope: immigration is the worked example. The distinctions also apply to research on technology, education, family policy, health systems and institutions; this memo does not claim that their substantive evidence has been audited. The [existing main question](immigration-main-question-reset.md) already asks about explicit populations, horizons, counterfactuals and incidence. The gap is executing that framing with appropriate evidence, rather than inventing another framework. [SOURCE: GOALS.md and current topic memos.]

## 1. What greater certainty should mean

Separate five claims: **the number is correctly constructed; the sample represents the named people; a specified cause changes the outcome; the effect transfers to the target setting; and the resulting trade-off is desirable under stated values.** Evidence can be strong at one level and weak at the next. “Verified” should name which level passed. A change in the population's average is also distinct from change experienced by the original people. [METHOD / INFERENCE]

The National Academies distinguishes rerunning the same computation, replication with independently obtained data, and generalization to other settings. Even exact computational reproduction does not guarantee correctness. Our NLS file joins and independently recomputed cells improve computational and measurement confidence; they are not new cohorts independently confirming a population mechanism. [SOURCE: [National Academies, 2019 summary](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/25303/chapter/3), definitions and reproducibility limitations.]

For a causal question about an intervention, specify the policy, eligible population, comparison policy, start of follow-up, outcomes and horizon before choosing the available dataset. The target-trial framework is a useful discipline when its components can be mapped to the data; it cannot manufacture missing information or eliminate confounding by naming the design. Historical comparison, measurement studies and normative arguments require their own evidence, rather than pretending every question is an intervention trial. [SOURCE: [Hernán et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39961105/), abstract and scope; INFERENCE for cross-domain application.]

## 2. Specific examples where cleaner statements matter

| Tempting statement | Defensible object and remaining question |
|---|---|
| “This group costs $8,300 per year.” | The 2024-income-year extended *partial* modeled account for adults 25–64 shows approximately +$7,698 for Mexican G2 and +$15,984 for its third-plus NH-white reference under equal-all-members allocation. Their difference is −$8,286. That difference, an absolute total fiscal balance, and the effect of admitting another migrant are three distinct quantities. These figures do not establish any complete balance's sign. [S1] |
| “Later generations do not assimilate.” | Pew measures different patterns for English, perceived similarity to a typical American, party affiliation and policy preferences. Name the outcome and comparator. Cross-sectional generation patterns do not track the same families through successive generations. [S2] |
| “Generation increases crime.” | Our repaired NLS categories give different arrest and incarceration patterns, small event cells and substantial unresolved history. Next ask whether a defined contrast can distinguish a materially important difference after measurement, selection and age exposure are handled. [S3] |
| “Immigration does not affect fertility.” | The repaired metro estimate is imprecise and the proposed instrument is weak. The outcome concerns a recent birth report, rather than completed family size. A null effect has not been established. [S4] |
| “Less automation means a welfare loss.” | Labor-saving invention, total investment, productivity, prices and who benefits are different links. Evidence about the first link does not price the final one; a return of annual invention effects toward zero does not establish catch-up in accumulated knowledge. [S4] |
| “The AFQT gap is precisely x standard deviations.” | The held test-score lane explicitly scales percentile-point coefficients using an approximate constant. A standardized mean gap cannot be recovered from a mean percentile gap alone. Preserve source-scale results; reconstruct the intended standardized metric from suitable distributions before citing precise SD magnitudes. Transforming group means is not equivalent to averaging transformed individual scores. [S5; mathematical measurement inference] |

These examples identify different failure modes. They are not evidence that every original direction reverses: some measured differences survive corrections, while the broader inference remains unresolved. [INFERENCE]

## 3. Domain map: endpoints and discriminating evidence

The questions below are proposed research targets, not new empirical findings. The final three rows extend beyond the domains deeply audited in this turn; they identify coverage gaps without claiming an exhaustive absence-of-literature search. [METHOD]

| Domain | Sharper question | Evidence that would add substance |
|---|---|---|
| **Living standards and work** | Under a specified entry/work-access policy, whose real disposable income, employment and leisure change, and for how long? | Follow incumbents rather than changing resident averages; separate hours, job loss, prices and rents; use a relevant policy shock and check exits. QWI alone cannot supply native hourly wages. [S6] |
| **Public finances and services** | For one admission cohort and horizon, which governments receive revenue, incur marginal costs or deliver worse services? | Align taxpayers, dependants, time, taxes and costs; distinguish average allocations from marginal spending; add delivery outcomes such as wait times or classroom capacity. Annual stock gaps cannot simply become lifetime admission effects. [S1,S7] |
| **Housing and urban life** | How do inflows affect incumbent renters, owners, construction, crowding and travel once supply responds? | Same-place demand and construction exposure, usable capacity, incumbent outcomes and pretrends; financing plus person-nights/pupil-days, not spending divided by a mismatched population count. [S7] |
| **Safety and justice** | How does a policy change victimization severity and frequency, alongside enforcement and punishment? | Separate self-report, reports to police, arrests, convictions and custody; align person-time and age; retain unknown status/origin; test recording and selection explanations. Descendant categories cannot be borrowed from another dataset. [S3,S8] |
| **Education and capabilities** | Which family/school conditions change skills and adult opportunities, including descendants who no longer identify with an origin label? | Repair score units; follow held NLS education/earnings outcomes using repaired family histories; examine test-taking/retention; replicate comparable constructs in another cohort. Education/language adjustment answers a conditional question and may remove part of the pathway of interest. [S3,S5] |
| **Health and family life** | Which exposures change healthy functioning, family stability and realized family-size preferences? | For disability, pool appropriate years with sample-overlap-aware uncertainty and direct generation contrasts. For fertility, distinguish births, timing, completed fertility and residential sorting; verify event counts and denominators before a causal design. Benefit eligibility/receipt is a separate endpoint. [S4] |
| **Innovation and long-run production** | Does labor supply change the accumulated stock of useful innovation and its benefits beyond affected firms? | Cumulative patent-count responses with uncertainty; competing types of invention; follow-up on productivity, prices and spillovers from the same shock. Firm investment alone does not answer this. [S4] |
| **Trust, cooperation and institutions** | Which conditions alter actual cooperation, impartial enforcement and government performance? | Comparable attitude items plus observed participation/cooperation and service outcomes; behavioral or policy evidence that can separate composition from institutional change. A vote shift is not itself evidence of improved or degraded institutions. [S2,S4,S6] |
| **Culture, meaning and continuity** | Which traditions, practices, languages and ways of life persist or change; what do people value about those changes? | Explicitly chosen cultural objects; longitudinal participation/intergenerational transmission; archival and comparative cases, including counterexamples. Distinguish descriptions of change from arguments about excellence, belonging or loss. A tax or trust coefficient cannot settle that evaluation. [FRAMING-SENSITIVE; proposed scope] |
| **Freedom, legitimacy and resilience** | How do policies affect usable rights, arbitrary coercion, democratic accountability and ability to withstand shocks? | Concrete institutional outcomes and episodes, enforcement records, negative cases and credible comparisons; risk scenarios separated from estimated frequencies. “Civilizational decline” needs specified failure modes before it can be tested. [FRAMING-SENSITIVE; proposed scope] |
| **Environment and physical capacity** | Which policy changes resource use, pollution, congestion and exposure locally and globally? | Separate redistribution of people from changed total consumption/technology; actual infrastructure bottlenecks and displacement/spillover effects. This was not estimated in the current survey work. [proposed scope] |

## 4. The next work, ranked by the gap it can close

These priorities are judgment calls about information value, not numerical forecasts of research success. A new dataset earns priority when its missing variables or design can change a named conclusion. [INFERENCE]

1. **Finish a bounded reliability pass on the load-bearing claims.** Recompute unvalidated test-score conversions or report original units; make absolute-account/benchmark-gap distinctions explicit; attach current corrections directly to old headline uses. For NLS, determine which variance/design information is actually available, report honest uncertainty, and quantify sensitivity to unknown family history and retention. A wide interval cannot be repaired by stronger prose. Existing data suffice for parts; exact Mexico-specific grandparent regions remain restricted. [S1,S3,S5]
2. **Follow family outcomes rather than assemble disconnected generation snapshots.** Join the already-held NLS education/earnings outcomes to repaired family history by validated PUBID; preserve generic migration categories and self-ID separately. Show classified/unresolved and retained/missing outcomes, and analyze one-versus-two foreign-born-parent histories where valid. Establish which plausible selection patterns would erase or reverse a result. This improves descendant descriptions and mechanism tests; it does not randomize ancestry. Access to country-specific history would resolve a more specific question than another generic NLS export. [S3]
3. **Complete one policy-relevant causal comparison end to end.** Pick an intervention whose margin matches the question—work authorization, a visa allocation, an admission change or a local capacity response. Reproduce treatment assignment/first stage and directly measured endpoints; inspect pretrends, spillovers, composition and competing channels. Enforce the distinction between admission and removal, short-run firm outcomes and long-run household welfare. A stronger design for the wrong policy is still the wrong answer. [S6; methodological recommendation]
4. **Complete one joint local incidence case.** Measure housing and service quantity/quality alongside spending in one place and period, separately for incumbent renters, owners, competing workers and taxpayers. State which effects are identified and which are accounting or descriptive. This can establish a concrete gain/loss distribution even if a national all-effects total remains unknown. [S7]
5. **Resolve the long-run mechanism with the greatest plausible decision impact.** Automation is a candidate because the invention mechanism has evidence while the net long-run consequence remains open. Start with cumulative invention and follow-up endpoints from the same shock; stop before monetary extrapolation if the chain is unbridged. Native completed fertility is another potentially important frontier, but the current geography, outcome and instrument defects make another panel coefficient a poor immediate substitute. [S4]
6. **Test transfer and competing explanations before scaling the conclusion.** Ask whether a result survives an independent cohort, a different instrument or an institution with a predicted contrasting response. Different designs with different vulnerabilities add more than many specifications sharing the same selection. A cross-national comparison is useful only after outcome, population and institutional differences are made explicit. [S6; National Academies source above]

Two inexpensive social-measurement extensions belong in the first tranche: estimate direct disability contrasts across compatible ASEC years with overlap-aware uncertainty, and show GSS trust distributions including the currently excluded “depends” response alongside the existing definite-response estimand. The latter is a denominator sensitivity, not a presumption that the current sign reverses. The inspected GSS code defines trust only for codes1/2 and uses clustered weighted-regression errors; availability of strata/PSU fields does not mean those errors already implement the full survey design. [SOURCE: [GSS loader](../infra/immigration-fiscal/attitudes_gen_2026_09_16/gss_gen.py), [adjusted estimator](../infra/immigration-fiscal/attitudes_gen_2026_09_16/gss_adj.py), S4.]

Restricted data are worth pursuing when their contents would distinguish live hypotheses: inclusive country-specific lineage with adult outcomes; administrative earnings/tax linkage; or valid individual service exposure. Complete LNS2006 data would improve some attitude work but would not by itself identify contemporary political consequences or causal assimilation. Do not retry unchanged ICPSR access or buy data merely to enlarge the inventory. [INFERENCE; S2,S3]

## 5. Stronger broad judgments without false precision

For an overall judgment, first state whose welfare matters, over what period, and how losses to different people and future generations are treated. Present material outcomes, safety, autonomy, family, culture and resilience in their own units until a conversion is justified. Some disagreements will remain disagreements about values after every empirical fact is agreed. That is a substantive conclusion, not a measurement failure. [FRAMING-SENSITIVE]

When aggregation is justified, use a consistent population, horizon, price basis and accounting convention; separate transfers from resource costs and prevent overlapping valuation of productivity, income, tax receipts, avoidance and asset prices. Unmeasured components stay unestimated, not zero. If a common-unit measured balance is B, ask how large the unmeasured components would have to be to change its sign. Such a break-even calculation is conditional; it is not an empirical bound without justified limits on those components. For incommensurable outcomes, report trade-offs and which preferences reverse the ranking. [METHOD; S1,S4,S6]

A useful statement should therefore answer: **What is observed? For whom and when? Compared with what? What inference does the design permit? What important alternative survives? What observation would change the statement?** Use that compact form in the existing evidence system rather than building another checklist or database for its own sake. The existing project already contains detailed methodological rules. [INFERENCE]

## Analyze closeout

- **Leading explanation:** uncertainty persists mainly because claims cross population, measurement, causal and welfare levels faster than the evidence does. Recent repairs of parent routing, political denominators, fiscal interpretations and causal chains support this diagnosis. [S1–S5]
- **Top alternative:** some questions mainly need missing data or larger valid samples. Exact country lineage, sparse NLS events and administrative service exposures are real limits that cleaner wording cannot remove. [S3,S7]
- **Discriminating evidence:** a fixed-scope reanalysis and an independent policy-relevant design that agree despite different biases would strengthen the substantive conclusion. If correctly defined estimates still reverse under reasonable selection or transport assumptions, that uncertainty is the result. Failure to narrow those assumptions would favor a data/identification ceiling over another precision cleanup.
- **Decision impact:** prioritize claim-critical missing links and outcome-relevant designs; retain useful descriptions at their proper level; separate cultural/normative judgment from empirical effect estimates.
- **Next action:** the first bounded tranche should be score-unit validation plus NLS uncertainty/selection and descendant education/earnings follow-up. In parallel, select one policy comparison and one local incidence case by actual data availability. This memo does not claim those new analyses are completed or silently authorize account changes, paid acquisition or external publication.
