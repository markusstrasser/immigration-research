## New frameworks: conditions for reuse

| Framework | What the available evidence supports | Missing bridge |
|---|---|---|
| Clemens–Pritchett calibration | Reproduce the published model; show parameter sensitivity as exploratory. | Its assimilation parameter concerns productivity apart from observed education. Raw earnings, fiscal and attitude gaps are not direct estimates of that parameter. Cross-sectional stock assimilation is relevant in the paper, so “not longitudinal” alone is not a valid objection; mapping the repo's particular cohorts, skills and outcomes still needs justification. Do not treat zero convergence on a political preference as a bound on productivity assimilation. [Paper §6.2](https://www.cgdev.org/sites/default/files/CGD-Working-Paper-423-Clemens-Pritchett-New-Econ-Case-Migration_0.pdf) |
| Neighborhood price capitalization | Test conditional price/rent associations and, with valid identification, price responses to neighborhood change. | A bundled price change does not isolate ethnicity preferences or national welfare loss. State whose welfare is counted, separate owner/tenant incidence, and avoid adding both capitalized stock loss and its rental flow or re-adding school/crime amenities already priced elsewhere. The brief acknowledges bundled amenities; these further restrictions govern dollar reuse. [Current brief](../infra/immigration-fiscal/hedonic_composition_2026_09_19/BRIEF.md) [INFERENCE] |
| Generational accounting | Report birthplace, parental birthplace and self-identification separately; include descendants when the question requires them. | A self-identified third-plus cross-section is not a full lineage forecast. Selection on earnings does not bound every fiscal or attitude outcome. |

These are conditions on interpretation, not a replacement of the project's causal protocol or permission to invent missing parameters. ## Primary-source and arithmetic checks

- **Fiscal consolidation:** [Census glossary](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/state/about/glossary.html) and [FHWA 2024 FA-5](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/policyinformation/statistics/2024/fa5.cfm). The latter reports $60.999bn to states and $2.8666bn to local governments. This establishes an overlap channel; different fiscal vintages and included functions prevent treating $63.8656bn as the exact account correction.
- **Population average versus incumbents:** [CBO February 2024, Box 2-1, printed p.50](https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2024-02/59710-Outlook-2024.pdf), and [Decker's actual argument](https://nicholasdecker.substack.com/p/yes-immigrants-must-make-us-richer). Formal counterexample: 100 incumbents rise from 100 to 101; add 20 newcomers earning 50. Every incumbent gains while the population average falls to 92.5. [CALCULATION]
- **Schooling-loan claim:** [Tabarrok's original post](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2015/09/open-borders-and-welfare.html). Its assurance is stronger than established here; the benchmark-gap rebuttal does not adjudicate it.
- **Prices:** [Kim–Leung–Weinberger, February 2026, abstract and introduction](https://ryansungryongkim.github.io/papers/KLW_25Feb2026.pdf) describes a goods-price benefit through demand/search. [Barrett–Tan, IMF WP25/5](https://www.imf.org/-/media/Files/Publications/WP/2025/English/wpiea2025005-print-pdf.ashx) reports an imprecise other-services effect. No full replication of either paper was performed.
- **Crime definitions:** [Light, He and Robey, Materials and Methods](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2014704117) and [BJS Criminal Victimization 2024](https://bjs.ojp.gov/document/cv24.pdf); independent NCVS annualization and row-share arithmetic in the crime evidence.
- **School experiments:** official [2010 DC evaluation](https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/evaluation-report/evaluation-impact-dc-opportunity-scholarship-program-final-report) and [later SOAR evaluation](https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/evaluations/evaluation-effectiveness-scholarships-opportunity-and-results-act-soar-program). The official evaluation records were checked; no claim of full reanalysis of the experiments.
- **Executable audit probes:** [fiscal probe](../notes/audit-2026-09-19/fiscal_probe.py), [IV-scale probe](../notes/audit-2026-09-19/iv_scale_probe.py). They use existing inputs and leave principal analysis outputs unchanged. Recorded numerical results are in the linked evidence notes.
