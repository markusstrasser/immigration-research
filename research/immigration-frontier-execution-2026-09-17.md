# Executed frontier: which conclusions now carry more weight?

**Verdict:** Some descriptive gaps survive materially better tests; a single story of generational improvement or decline does not. The main gains are survey-design uncertainty, direct family-history sensitivity, compatible multi-year health comparisons, a spending/exposure account, and sharper separation of policy effects from group averages. The original test-score SD conversion is unsupported. These results strengthen specific statements without establishing an overall immigration benefit or harm across every domain of life. [DATA / INFERENCE; sources below.]

This executes the [September17 research frontier](immigration-research-question-frontier-2026-09-17.md), using immigration as the worked example. All supplied datasets were already inventoried in the [named library](../infra/immigration-fiscal/new_datasets_2026_09_17/library/README.md); this tranche extends analysis rather than implying another set of ZIPs is independent evidence. Raw sources remain unchanged; respondent outputs remain local and ignored. [SOURCE]

## 1. Family history, ability, education and crime

The official NLS AFQT benchmark reproduces: mean 50.4099447 and survey-design SE 0.6376814, versus official 50.410/0.638. This establishes that the public 117-stratum/234-pseudo-PSU variance implementation works on its principal benchmark. It does not remove selective testing, interview attrition or classification error. The analysis joins all 8,984 IDs and independently checks adult degree/wage fields against the held outcome cache. [N]

Duncan et al.'s primary Table 8 coefficients are −31.75/−24.98/−17.72/−25.63 **percentile points** for 1.5/G2/G3/G4+ relative to their fourth-plus white comparator. A common multiplier does not convert these to raw-score or IQ standard deviations. The paper's restricted origin variables, sample and adjusted model differ from our public calculation; disagreement between the two is not a replication failure. Individually calculated percentile-SD and rank-normal alternatives are retained under those exact names, without relabeling them IQ. [N; [published source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10836839/).]

Our public regular-HS-or-higher rates are 88.01% in G3 and 78.12% in G4+; the direct difference is −9.90 pp [−18.73, −1.06]. But the pool with both biological parents reported US-born and unresolved grandparents has only 53.23% completion among observed outcomes. Allocating that pool to G3 changes the rates to 75.64%/78.12%, reversing their point ordering. Equality occurs in a uniform fractional-allocation scenario with 85.74% assigned to G3 and the remaining 14.26% to G4+. This is a sensitivity threshold, not inferred ancestry. Degree outcomes are from 2013; missing wages and unknown parent histories are not zeros. [N]

The identity measure also selects on assessment: all 897 ASVAB Mexican/Chicano self-identifiers have AFQT, while 752 of 4,334 baseline-white comparison respondents lack it. This blocks a casual interpretation of the score table as covering every Mexican-origin descendant. Within public G2, one foreign-born plus one US-born parent has a 9.98-point higher AFQT mean than two foreign-born parents [3.62, 16.34], demonstrating heterogeneity without identifying its cause. [N]

Common-age histories improve the crime comparison but leave wide uncertainty. Mexican-self-ID men's adult-facility incarceration between age 12 and the month before 30 is 14.74%/14.71%/10.56% in G2/G3/G4+, based on 16/113, 7/46 and 7/46 observed events/respondents. G4+−G3 is −4.15 pp [−17.71, 9.41]. These are reported custody histories among retained respondents, not victimization or offending rates. Small event cells cannot establish parity or a monotonic trend. [N]

## 2. Health, trust and mechanisms

The disability contrast survives separate-sex and broad birth-cohort comparisons, weakening a pure age/sex-composition explanation. Migration selection, reporting differences and identity selection remain live alternatives. The three ASEC files overlap substantially, so their estimates are averaged with a worst-case covariance SE bound rather than treated as three independent samples. G2−white is−1.32pp[−3.06,0.42]; G3+self-ID−white+1.41pp[−0.38,3.20]. Neither supports an established reversal relative to whites. [S]

Public/private income separation matters: G2 averages $701 in Social Security reported with a disability reason plus SSI, versus $767 for the white comparison; the difference is −$66 [−$339, $208]. This is reported income, not administrative SSDI expenditure. Occupational, private-insurance and unknown-source payments remain separate. [S]

GSS trust gaps persist after the relevant measurement corrections. But the adjusted G3+−G1 change is +2.08 pp [−2.36, 6.52]; “the gap is precisely flat across generations” is too strong. Education-adjusted attitudes answer a conditional question and do not establish immigration's total effect on cooperation or government. [S]

The [institutions/contact/environment follow-up](../infra/immigration-fiscal/frontier_execution_2026_09_17/social/institutions-environment.md) tests a more useful rival to fixed group explanations: institutions and settings can change specific behavior. Swiss citizenship evidence distinguishes political knowledge/efficacy from trust; randomized mixed soccer teams in Iraq change some within-sport choices without clearly generalizing off the field. This is evidence about particular interventions, not a warrant to assume either complete cultural convergence or immutable differences. [SOURCE: [Swiss study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4611668/), [Mousa experiment](https://poverty-action.org/sites/default/files/publications/Building-social-cohesion-between-Christians-and-Muslims-through-soccer-in-post-ISIS-Iraq.pdf); INFERENCE.]

## 3. Local burdens and incomplete fiscal accounts

NYC's gross accrued asylum-service costs fall$3.752bn→$3.020bn while approximate census person-days fall23.37m→18.68m. Program spending per census-day is$160.55→$161.68. The city-funded portion falls faster than gross costs, so financing transfers and real resource savings must remain separate. The two official population charts disagree slightly beyond rounding; both are retained and yield the same substantive result. These figures are neither hotel-night prices nor a national net-immigration account. [L]

Meyer, Wyse and Williams'2026 paper supports an additional mechanism: much of the recent increase in sheltered homelessness consists of new arrivals themselves entering shelter. Its direct NYC count is66,700 against a77,352 increase in the sheltered PIT count; the alternative indirect construction gives a smaller share. This is a real concentrated service burden. It does not prove that the same number of incumbents lost homes. Housing scarcity, crowding and displaced demand still require their own evidence. The2024 shelter-rule audit lacked systematic post-exit housing/school outcomes, so fewer sheltered people cannot be assumed to mean fewer people needing help. [L; [published paper](https://bpb-us-w2.wpmucdn.com/voices.uchicago.edu/dist/a/3122/files/2026/02/Meyer-Wyse-Williams-JPubE-2026.pdf).]

The fiscal reconciliation supplies explicit break-even statements. Under the all-member allocation, omitted net fiscal components would need to equal−$7,698 per adult-year to bring the modeled Mexican-G2 balance to zero. By contrast, omitted components would need to favorG2 over its white reference by$8,286 to erase that benchmark gap. Those are different unknowns. Neither threshold is an empirical bound, and neither authorizes lifetime multiplication or adding overlapping school/shelter costs again. [F]

## 4. Policy interventions, production and invention

The Bracero analysis reconstructs exposure and wages from 15,831 raw rows. Four hourly/daily wage specifications reproduce the sample sizes and printed point estimates in the authors' July 2017 working paper; a second numerical method independently verifies the coefficients. The all-years hourly coefficient is −$0.0356 in 1965 dollars per full 0-to-1 baseline exposure contrast, with a 95% interval [−$0.1219, +$0.0506]. Exposure is the mean 1955 monthly share of hired seasonal workers who were Mexican. This supports the narrow conclusion that ending this agricultural guest-worker program did not generate its model's predicted substantial wage gains in more exposed states. It does not establish an exact zero. [P; [primary paper](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w23125/w23125.pdf).]

Two limits are consequential. The raw CSV came from a public third-party mirror whose GitHub object hashes verify, but whose identity with the official Stata package remains unverified. The domestic-employment specifications fail to reproduce the source's sample and coefficients and are explicitly excluded from confirmed evidence. Calling this a full official replication would be false. [P]

The final July 2026 H-2B lottery paper supplies a different policy setting: winning priority predicts hiring and approximately 14.5% higher conditional geometric revenue among the observed firms. Its US-worker hiring intervals include negative effects. The sample conditions on survey response and survival, covers 3–9 months, and defines US workers as citizens plus lawful permanent residents. Business stealing can separate a firm's gain from the effect of expanding the national quota. We verified primary tables and arithmetic; the raw package remains inaccessible in this environment. [P; [published reprint](https://www.piie.com/sites/default/files/2026-07/wp26-11.pdf).]

Danzer's automation result concerns patent counts as well as patent shares. For a 10-percentage-point increase in its allocation-to-baseline ratio, the year-4 count coefficient implies a conditional ratio of 0.694 [0.526, 0.916]. The ratio's numerator is centrally allocated ethnic Germans; its denominator is the pre-allocation stock of unskilled manual workers plus unemployed people. It is not an observed change in employed migrants. The regions begin allocation in 1996, 1997 or 2002. [P; [published supplement, B-6](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0047272724000720-mmc1.pdf).]

Annual effects approaching zero do not replace missing past inventions. Conversely, the published coefficients do not establish a precisely measured cumulative loss: untreated fitted counts, aggregation weights and joint uncertainty are missing. An equal-year geometric-average ratio calculation gives 0.812, with a covariance-conservative interval [0.629, 1.047]. This is **not** a cumulative count or stock estimate. Positive firm investment, agricultural adjustment after exclusion and reduced local automation invention can coexist; none alone settles total welfare. [P; INFERENCE]

## 5. Use family migration history, with identity kept separate

For this research, the useful representation is a **family migration history**:

- Own birthplace and arrival age/year.
- Each parent's birthplace, preserving one-parent/two-parent and unknown-other-parent cases.
- Each grandparent's birthplace where actually recorded; country unknown stays unknown.
- Self-identification, language and cultural practice as separate outcomes or predictors.

“US-born; one Mexico-born parent; other parent US-born” and “US-born with two US-born parents and one recorded Mexico-born grandparent” are meaningful nonracial descriptions. “One of four grandparents was born in Mexico” is not a DNA measurement, and country of birth is not itself a race. A fractional allocation used to avoid double-counting mixed backgrounds is an accounting convention. If two grandparents are unobserved, do not call one observed Mexico-born grandparent exactly25% ancestry. [METHOD / INFERENCE]

CPS can support parent-country categories; public NLS supports generic foreign/US grandparent categories; neither supplies the same inclusive country-specific lineage. Keep mixed origins and unresolved branches rather than force every person into one label. The existing self-ID-based third-plus category remains labeled as such. [SOURCE: [family-history audit](immigration-new-conclusions-audit-2026-09-17.md), [NLS reconstruction](immigration-nlsy97-parent-linkage-2026-09-17.md).]

## Evidence status and interpretation

The leading explanation for earlier overreach is a mixture of distinct outcomes, populations and causal levels. A rival explanation—real persistent disadvantage obscured by noisy measurement—survives for several endpoints. The strongest discriminators are now clear: inclusive origin-specific family histories, observed outcomes after service exit, and credible policy variation with response/selection checked. Existing descriptive gaps should not be discarded merely because they are not causal; causal and welfare claims need additional evidence. [INFERENCE]

Culture, freedom, legitimacy, environmental quality and resilience require named outcomes and explicit value judgments. For carbon, an immigrant's assigned destination footprint is not the global emissions added by migration: origin consumption, remittances and supply-chain changes belong in the counterfactual. For culture, a lower trust answer, a vote shift, language acquisition and preservation of a valued practice are different objects. No single monetary or “civilizational” score has been established here. [METHOD / FRAMING-SENSITIVE; institutions/environment follow-up.]

## What would now change the conclusions

| Domain | Sharper question | Missing discriminating evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Education, earnings and crime | At comparable ages, how do inclusive country-specific family histories predict each outcome, including people who no longer self-identify? | Origin-specific linkage, unresolved-family histories and outcome nonresponse; small crime cells also need more events. |
| Health | Does the descendant disability difference persist under a second instrument and within comparable arrival/occupational histories? | Administrative or independently measured function and linked migration/occupation histories. More ASEC years alone do not remove reporting or selection bias. |
| Housing and local services | Which incumbent renters, owners and pupils gain or lose when arrivals interact with housing/school capacity? | Outcomes after shelter exit, rental exposure and actual district capacity; gross spending is only one component. |
| Fiscal accounts | What is the complete annual balance, and separately what changes under a specific admission policy? | Missing tax/service components and marginal responses, with a common household/person universe. The present thresholds are bookkeeping, not bounds. |
| Wages and firms | Does the same policy contrast change incumbent earnings, firm survival and prices beyond the surveyed survivors? | H-2B raw assignment/response data and the official Bracero sample code; the latter's employment replication is unresolved. |
| Innovation | Does labor allocation change cumulative automation inventions and their downstream use? | Counterfactual fitted counts and joint covariance or raw estimation data; inventor location alone does not measure diffusion. |
| Politics, trust and institutions | Which institutional changes alter concrete cooperation or participation, and does that generalize beyond the setting? | Swiss author data were not located; the bounded access attempt is [recorded](../infra/immigration-fiscal/frontier_execution_2026_09_17/social/swiss/RESULT.md). Existing experimental contact evidence shows limited transfer. |
| Environment and culture | What changes globally or locally under the migration counterfactual, and which cultural outcomes do we value? | Origin/destination consumption paths and spillovers; named cultural outcomes and an explicit evaluative frame. Destination footprints and identity labels alone cannot answer these questions. |

All six proposed workstreams were attempted. Measurement and descriptive follow-ups produced new verified results; the local incidence account and policy replications are partial for the reasons above. Fertility, agglomeration, Pew/LNS political results and previous fiscal components retain their earlier audited status; this tranche supplies no new independent estimates for them. An exhaustive causal account of all civilizational domains has not been completed. [COVERAGE]
