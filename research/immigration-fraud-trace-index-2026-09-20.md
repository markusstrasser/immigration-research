## Shirley's hospice leads and the strongest checks

His [September 10 newsletter](https://www.antifraudclub.com/p/25-years-after-9-11-america-still-comes-together) names four hospice businesses and asserts $170m questionable California billing and $30m at risk at one plaza. These remain attributed claims [C3]; exact address/license/NPI/payment matches have not been completed.

The linked [CMS May 13 release](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-announces-aggressive-nationwide-crackdown-fraud-six-month-hospice-home-health-agency-enrollment) verifies payment suspensions for approximately **800 Los Angeles hospice/home-health providers**, associated with **$1.4bn prior-year spending**, and **$70m funds suspended**. Those are provider counts, historical spending and withheld funds respectively, not three fraud-loss estimates. [A2]

[California's April 8 records check](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/news/california-cracks-down-on-hospice-fraud-in-los-angeles-debunks-viral-claims/) reports 19 licensed/Medi-Cal-eligible entities and 109 never licensed at a viral Van Nuys address. This challenges treating directory entries as active paid providers; its Medi-Cal scope does not clear Medicare billing. [A2, agency account] A hospice office's occupancy also differs from a day-care attendance test because care may occur elsewhere; physical observation must match the billed service. [SOURCE: [HHS-OIG hospice overview](https://oig.hhs.gov/reports/featured/hospice/).]

## Denominators and access

- **SNAP:** the [2015–17 USDA trafficking study](https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/resource-files/Trafficking2015-2017-Summary-1.pdf) estimated 1.6% of redemption dollars under its final-decision definition, versus 2.0% using the prior definition. These are old model estimates, not current prevalence or confidence limits. [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-19-167) identifies unvalidated assumptions and investigation-sample selection; the methodological recommendation remains open on the inspected page. Payment-error rates additionally include unintentional errors and underpayments. [A2]
- **Childcare:** [HHS-OIG's 2023 Minnesota payment audit](https://oig.hhs.gov/reports/all/2025/minnesota-could-better-ensure-that-childcare-assistance-providers-comply-with-attendance-requirements/) estimated 11% of payments had attendance/payment errors. This is neither 11% of dollars stolen nor a ceiling on undetected fraud. [A2]
- **Public tracing:** [USDA retailer histories](https://www.fna.usda.gov/snap/retailer-locator), state licenses, registries, [CMS hospice provider data methodology](https://www.cms.gov/research-statistics-data-and-systems/statistics-trends-and-reports/medicare-provider-charge-data/downloads/hospice_methodology.pdf) and court exhibits support entity/payment joins. Store-level SNAP redemptions face a confidentiality barrier documented in [FMI v. Argus Leader](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/18pdf/18-481_5426.pdf). The [HHS Medicaid HCPCS release](https://opendata.hhs.gov/datasets/medicaid-provider-spending) advertises January 2018–December 2024 outpatient/professional data; its full dictionary and suppression rules were not retrieved, so no complete-coverage claim or analysis is made. [GAP]

## Integration rule

[INFERENCE / ACCOUNTING] Already-paid fraud within included benefit-program totals is not another government outlay to add. It can materially change attribution: a patient assigned medical spending may have received no service while a provider kept the money. Correcting that requires tracing the actual funds, legitimate-service counterfactual and recoveries. Provider origin, patient origin and causal immigration-policy effects are separate questions. Case-selected evidence supplies neither an ethnic fraud rate nor a national loss estimate.

Each future row needs the allegation/video, entity and effective identifiers, program, payment dates, billed/paid amounts, established fraudulent portion, decision status, ordered/actual recoveries and shared scheme ID. This prevents counting a scheme again for each defendant, press release, retailer and recipient. The Future Leaders nutrition amount also overlaps Feeding Our Future; it cannot be added to that scheme's total again.

[GAP] This pass establishes real cases and executable tracing routes. It does not reconcile every Shirley provider, extract a national payment panel, or justify any upward/downward adjustment to the Mexican-origin annual result. Such an adjustment requires matched-year payments and supported origin allocation. Both dismissing documented fraud and labeling all suspicious spending as theft fail the evidence test.
