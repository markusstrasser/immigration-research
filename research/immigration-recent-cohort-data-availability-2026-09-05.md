## What would resolve the Somali / newer-cohort question

The direct descriptive contrast is **2019 ACS residents with entry years 2016–2019 versus 2024 ACS residents with entry years 2021–2024**, with the same age range, equal duration-in-US bins, replicate-weight uncertainty, origin categories and common-dollar conversion. Within each snapshot, separately show Somalia-born (`POBP=448`), all recent foreign-born residents and all foreign-born residents; show weighted denominators and unweighted counts before reporting percentages. Report population composition and labor outcomes separately, then standardize age/sex/education/origin when those are explicitly part of the question. A aggregate “new cohort is worse” claim is falsified if its chosen outcome does not worsen under the prespecified comparable population; the reverse claim deserves the identical check. [INFERENCE: falsifiable descriptive design]

This comparison **does not identify a Biden-policy effect**. Entry cohorts differ in pandemic exposure, selection, survival/emigration, duration and macro conditions; a cross-section omits people who left, died, were missed or had not yet arrived when interviewed. Annual `YOEP` gives coarse duration, and equal four-year bins need not have equal within-bin arrival distributions. Employment is current status; ACS earnings/income cover a rolling prior 12 months and can partly precede US arrival for new arrivals. ADJINC adjusts within-vintage income to that vintage's dollars; an additional explicit price conversion is required across 2019/2024. None of these incomes is automatically US taxable earnings. [SOURCE: ACS dictionary and survey timing; INFERENCE: interpretation]

“Somalia-born,” “Somali ancestry,” “principal applicant nationality Somalia,” “Muslim,” and “extremist” are **different constructs**. Birthplace omits ethnic Somalis born elsewhere and includes people regardless of their beliefs; ancestry can include US-born descendants. The inspected ACS fields and RPC tables do not measure ideology or extremist conduct. Such claims require their own defined outcomes and individual-level evidence. Nor is a small subgroup's outcome an estimate for all post-2020 entrants. [DATA: inspected variable/column scope; INFERENCE: construct validity]
