# Mexico-born arrival cohorts: did selection fall across cohorts? (2026-09-18)

**Verdict:** PROBE IN PROGRESS [UNVERIFIED]

Purpose: test on local IPUMS ACS/Census microdata plus staged ACS 2024 1-year PUMS whether earlier
Mexico-born (BPL==200) arrival cohorts show higher measured skill (education, English, wage residual
vs US-born non-Hispanic whites) than later cohorts including 2021–2024 arrivals, bounded for
return-migration selection (ladder 92).
