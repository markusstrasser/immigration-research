# Institutions and liberal-democratic norms by generation — Hispanic/Mexican-origin vs native non-Hispanic white

**Verdict:** PROBE IN PROGRESS [UNVERIFIED]

Purpose: measure GSS institutional confidence, the Stouffer civil-liberties tolerance battery, and rule-of-law/civic items by Hispanic and Mexican-origin generation (G1/G2/G3+) against non-Hispanic whites overall and against white subgroups by ideology and education, with ANES democratic-norms items and external anchors (WVS7, LAPOP, Pew).
