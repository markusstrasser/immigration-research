## Misuse risk

“Self-pay increased” can be wrongly cited as “undocumented people paid the hospital bill,” “hospitals lost $10m in real resources,” “public charge saved taxpayers $10m,” or “the policy caused more ED use.” The limiting definitions are “whether the primary source of payment ... was recorded as selfpay” and “payments are limited by their financial capacity”. The outcome is payer coding among encounters, not collections, total treatment use or net fiscal balance. The explicit warning “unmeasured confounding cannot be fully ruled out” also prevents claiming the isolated announcement's exact causal effect.
