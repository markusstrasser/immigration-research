## Design, identifying assumptions and threats

County fixed effects remove stable place differences; all predictors are lagged one wave, with a lagged dependent variable and robust SE. The authors report Hausman preference for FE over RE, VIF generally below3 (population4.61, mean2.13). They name omitted small counties, small N, possible differential nonreporting and county aggregation too broad for neighborhood mechanisms. They do not estimate the posited revitalization mediators or identify individual criminal behavior. [SOURCE: Analytical Strategy; Notes4; Discussion]

Additional concerns: lagging predictors does not make migration exogenous; three usable waves with a lagged outcome raise short-panel dynamic-FE bias; no wave fixed effects are shown or clearly described to absorb a statewide crime trend; control variables can mediate effects; 18 immigration×outcome tests and six separate models create multiple-comparison scope. Stable county FE do not eliminate changing enforcement/reporting or common trends. These are unresolved design threats, not claims to have reestimated or disproved coefficients. [INFERENCE from specified models]
