## Design, assumptions and threats

Descriptive accounting with within-metro/grade comparisons; no instrument, policy discontinuity or causal treatment. Estimate the enrollment-weighted average spending/need exposure for each race, compute minority/White ratios within grade/metro, then aggregate using minority pupil weights. Need-adjusted school dollars are `Y/(1+w_FRPL·FRPL+w_ELL·ELL)`. Weights are externally estimated and constant nationally: low (0.33,0.10), median (0.50,0.16), high (1.25,0.92). Special education is omitted from adjustment, because measurement differs across cost studies and observed race differences are small. [SOURCE: Measures; equation 2 original; C]

Assumptions: within-metro comparisons adequately control broad input prices, while selected weights cover compensating wage differentials inside metros; comparable normed spending; adequate FRPL imputation; transportability of cost functions across places; equal-expectations standard is the chosen fairness target. Authors explicitly describe results as illustrations rather than precise disparity measurements. No SE or CI for the main ratios is reported; low/mid/high is parameter sensitivity, not a sampling interval. [SOURCE: Measures; Appendix C; INFERENCE; FRAMING-SENSITIVE]
