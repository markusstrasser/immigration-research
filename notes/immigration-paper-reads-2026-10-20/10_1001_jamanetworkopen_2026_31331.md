## 8. Misuse risk and disconfirmation

“Our results indicate that such programs may partially offset their costs by reducing hospitals' uncompensated care burdens” is a hypothesis about partialoffsets, not a measured net saving. Authors explicitly warn: “we cannot fully rule out the influence of unmeasured state-level factors or differential state-specific trends that coincided with the policy implementation.” The strongest favorable mechanism is reimbursement replacing unpaidbills; the strongest statistical challenge is one treated state plus WBRI nonrejection for all mainATTS.
