## 3. Design, assumptions, author-named threats

Descriptive rates, difference-in-proportions tests; noinstrument or causal design. Assumes immigration classifiers match denominator populations, average resident exposure matches event/custody numerator, prisoner retention/caseprocessing do not differ materially across groups,and estimates account adequately for uncertainty. Groups overlap: unauthorized are a subset of foreigncitizens, not an independent comparator. No individual risk adjustment,matched-age denominator or standardization. Comparing average **offender** age29.29versus30.78 does not balance the **at-risk population** age distributions. [SOURCE: Incarceration Data/Results] [INFERENCE]

Authors' strongest case is that intentional homicide has high clearance and long sentences,so custody capture is better than for minoroffenses. They nevertheless state: “We have no way, therefore, of knowing the extent to which the estimates presented here would be biased to reflect enforcement as much as it reflects true offending differences.” They also name one-state/three-year scope,uncertain unauthorized denominators,counterfactual choice and the distinction from offending/arrest. This cannot establish a national Mexican-origin generation rate. [SOURCE: Discussion/Notes4,10,12]
