## 3. Identification, assumptions and threats

Wilcoxon rank-sum comparisons and linear regression; no policy discontinuity, instrument, longitudinal causal design or individual utilization observations. Need comparable reporting and correct denominators for descriptive interpretation. Cross-state associations reflect eligible population,age,diagnosis,coverage rules,reporting and prices; conditioning on expansion/ideology/region does not identify the causal cost of admission or removal.
