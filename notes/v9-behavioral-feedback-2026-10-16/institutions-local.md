## Implication for v9 and falsifiers

No additive dollars are warranted. Existing incomes, receipts and services already embody current behavior. A correction requires the *change* in nonmember behavior, prices, provision and financing under the declared counterfactual, while avoiding overlap with existing production, tax-gap, care and local-spending terms. v9 removes the whole Mexican-immigrant lineage including US-born descendants; citizens in enforcement studies are not automatically nonmember stayers. State/local transfers cancel within a consolidated national ledger, although their financing, service availability and distribution can matter. [INFERENCE/accounting]

For the local-loop claim, decisive evidence would follow preexisting residents/firms and governments jointly: an exogenous inflow or outflow, taxable income/property values, actual exits, services/quality, intergovernmental support, and further exits at a common horizon. A threshold should be estimated out of sample rather than inferred from one declining place. Stabilization of the tax base/services after initial exit, offsetting transfers, or no acceleration weakens the proposed self-reinforcing loop. This pass found partial fiscal responses, not that complete identification. [GAP / falsifier]
