## Additional scope limits retained

- Corporate capital incidence and average K–12/institutional costs depend on conventions (ladder 80). Attach the population, accounting scope, allocation and uncertainty to each number.
- Weighted cell population is not effective sample size. Schooling-factor sensitivity is in the findings memo. Primary standard errors assume zero cross-survey covariance; the reported dependence band addresses that assumption.
- The state decomposition ends in 2023. Population definitions and measurement methods change across evidence sources; compare like with like.
- Aging accounts for about −6% in the calendar-time custody comparison (ladder 70). Opportunity Atlas non-filing and other-Hispanic reallocation remain limits (ladder 82/65).
- The direct resident account is distinct from general-equilibrium effects and lifetime forecasts. Adult-ledger and legal-status figures are supported in ladder 76/85/119 and their lanes; missing them from a review packet did not invalidate them.
- Fourth-plus schooling stagnation does not contradict positive selection into ethnic attrition. Both findings can hold.
- The LLM instrument-bias caveat remains required by the project constitution. Model agreement was not treated as evidence.

The original disposition groups fiscal review items 1–82 and crime/generation items 1–78 into accepted corrections, existing source limitations and rejected objections. Reviewer IDs in the table retain that correspondence; raw speculative reviewer output and stylistic instructions are not source evidence.
