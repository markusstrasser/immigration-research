## Bansak, Pearlman and Sparber

Primary publisher record: *The impact of Secure Communities on the labor market outcomes of immigrant women*, Journal of Policy Analysis and Management 44(3), 917–942 (2025), first online 2024-10-27, DOI [10.1002/pam.22649](https://doi.org/10.1002/pam.22649).

The publisher abstract describes variation in deportation intensity by country of origin and city, and reports unchanged total employment alongside lower annual wages/hours and occupational changes. This is not a matched household-departure panel. The publisher's data statement routes deportation data and associated code to members-only TRAC, folder `tracfellows/Pearlman/JPAM2024`, and ACS data to IPUMS. TRAC's own [research note](https://tracreports.org/tracatwork/detail/A5879.html) describes deportation location/date/gender/origin and aggregation by country of origin and metropolitan area. [SOURCE]

Full main text and Appendix `pam22649-sup-0001-Appendix.pdf` were not obtained through the checked publisher routes. Consequently no numerical coefficient, uncertainty, or independent validation of the complete identification strategy is claimed. The author's [research page](https://irving.vassar.edu/faculty/sp/research.htm) lists an earlier title ending “Women Left Behind” without a manuscript link; that title does not establish actual partner departure. This remains an abstract/data-description assessment only. [SOURCE; LIMIT]

## Timing disconfirmation

Vargas Nunez et al. (2026), *Reconsideration of Secure Communities rollout reveals preemptive local-federal cooperation in immigration enforcement*, PNAS 123(15), e2510928123, [full primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13080013/), DOI [10.1073/pnas.2510928123](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2510928123). The article distinguishes states' MOA signing from counties' formal activation and finds pre-activation enforcement changes. This supplies a concrete anticipation/timing check for any future Secure Communities design. It does not itself estimate remaining-adult work, nor establish that every earlier enforcement estimate is invalid. No household response is taken from it. [SOURCE; INFERENCE]
