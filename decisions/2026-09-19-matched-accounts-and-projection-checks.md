# Keep observed accounts, matched benefits and forecast tests distinct

The user authorized national reconciliation, matched benefits and projection
back-testing. Census FY2024 finance is now available. The original NRC1997
report also explicitly publishes fiscal-policy alternatives; failure of its
debt rule cannot by itself establish failure of educational assimilation.

Use observed2024 state/local G/P and combined local/state C/X in a versioned
annual account. Preserve the former profile release for comparability. Report
BEA current saving and net borrowing separately, with remaining differences
unallocated. No per-capita distribution of unexplained national residuals.

For benefits, match the observed population and outside-group beneficiaries;
charge capital its alternative return and conserve private income plus taxes.
Large capital receipts require ownership and alternative-tax-location assumptions
and replace overlapping credits. They are not a free gross-income addition.

For projections, retain current profiles as conditional scenarios. Test observable
policy assumptions, perform a separately labeled temporal schooling test, and
expose recent-arrival, parentage and emigration sensitivities. Do not claim an
exact Mexican lifetime back-test without original forecast matrices and linked
cohort histories. The original century lane's complete-account and universally
favorable-exit wording is superseded; its historical calculations remain.

Evidence: [macro reconciliation](../research/immigration-macro-reconciliation-2026-09-19.md),
[matched benefits](../research/immigration-matched-benefits-2026-09-19.md), and
[projection tests](../research/immigration-projection-backtest-2026-09-19.md),
with pinned primary data, deterministic checks and independent code review.

Revisit with recipient-matched fiscal accounts, actual marginal ownership/tax
location and expenditure responses, or linked admission/parentage/exit/fiscal
records. Those could support stronger causal and lifetime claims.
