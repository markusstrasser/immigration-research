# Preserve measurements; correct their economic and ancestry interpretations

The user's two pasted summaries prompted a raw reconstruction of commit
`24e75ae`, an accounting-source audit and a generation-definition check.
The [audit memo](../research/immigration-aggregate-and-generation-audit-2026-09-17.md)
records source-level evidence, coverage and falsifiers.

Retain the all-age partial-account gaps of about $212bn/$153bn. Publish their
absolute partial balance (+$32bn) alongside them. Do not promote the hybrid
adult additions and nonfiscal crime valuations into a complete fiscal total.
Equal per-capita public-goods charges cancel in the stated benchmark contrast;
unpriced items do not have a predetermined sign.

Withdraw the purported $3.7bn/0.9% ceiling on US-born/G2 remittances: it assumes
the first-generation share that it claims to identify. Generation rates remain
scenario assumptions until independently measured. BEA's historical $69.94bn
line covers foreign-born senders, an additional source-scope correction.

Retain the IIMMLA/NLS/Pew percentages with their actual populations. Withdraw
the common exact-third-generation heading, Mexican-specific interpretation of
Pew's 77/50 percentages, precise negatively-selected-half claim, and universal
fifth-generation-impossibility claim. No source-coded IIMMLA generation is
automatically recoded from a single missing-history question.

The alternative of discarding the whole analysis is rejected: the baseline
arithmetic and many descriptive differences reproduce. The alternative of
accepting all interpretations because their inputs are real is rejected:
denominator and counterfactual changes are substantive errors.

Revisit on a consistent all-age extension with uncertainty, independently
measured generation-specific remittances, or inclusive country-lineage data.
This corrects conclusions within the existing research question; it does not
change the constitution, central question or causal protocol. Original evidence
is retained with dated correction pointers.
