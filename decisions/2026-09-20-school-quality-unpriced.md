# Reopen school-quality harm as unpriced

Alternatives considered: keep the zero (overstates what peer designs identify),
price a national loss from foreign evidence (unsupported transport), or retain an
unpriced channel with explicit resource-response scenarios (adopted).

The September16 review treated small or positive adjusted peer effects as proof
of no incumbent harm and booked $0. Primary-table review shows that several
designs absorb schoolwide resource changes or control staffing, so they cannot
exclude the capacity mechanism. US spending-cut evidence also finds harm among
white pupils, although it does not identify immigration's contribution.

Decision: preserve the evidence, withdraw the categorical zero and generation
exclusion, and label the national quality channel unpriced. Retain source-specific
peer estimates and conditional resource scenarios. Do not add a national dollar
cost without a matched arrival/resource/outcome design and nonoverlapping valuation.

Evidence and affected summaries:
[school-capacity note](../research/immigration-school-capacity-harms-2026-09-20.md),
ladder81, central generation/crime memo §13, September16 school-angle result and
bookmark-angle summary. This corrects an inference; it does not change the
conditional annual fiscal model or the central research question.

Revisit when a matched arrival/resource/incumbent-outcome design identifies a
transportable effect and a nonoverlapping valuation for the national beneficiary
population. Until then, no measured dollar addition or zero-effect claim follows.
