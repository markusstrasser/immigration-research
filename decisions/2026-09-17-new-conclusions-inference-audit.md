# 2026-09-17: Narrow new conclusions to the quantities actually measured

## Context

The user requested confirmation/disconfirmation of the latest conclusions and a better non-racial way to classify immigrant descendants. The five newest empirical lanes had moved from measurements to stronger causal, parity and welfare claims.

## Decision

Retain the descriptive disability gradient and evidence for labor-supply-driven technology choice. Withdraw the political dollar range as a bound, the Central Valley composition percentage, and purported no-effect conclusions drawn from imprecise nulls. Treat agglomeration and automation costs as unestimated rather than measured zero. Preserve original text as historical evidence and add explicit supersession pointers.

Prior position: ladder 93–97 and memo summaries. New evidence: raw-data recalculations, source-code/codebook discrepancies, San's explicit welfare exclusion, published Danzer patent-count estimates, and direct inspection of the monetary constructions. Threshold cleared: yes for correcting the specified claims; no for assigning opposite causal or policy verdicts. **PARTIAL-UPDATE.**

The proposed family-migration-history classification remains a recommendation. This decision does not change the central question, causal tree, analysis protocol, or existing raw group definitions.

## Alternatives considered

- Keep headlines and add generic caveats: rejected because several headlines contradict their own tables or exclude effects their intervals permit.
- Reverse every conclusion: rejected because substantial descriptive and design-specific findings reproduce.
- Correct the inference and identify the next discriminating measurement: selected.

## Evidence

[Adversarial audit](../research/immigration-new-conclusions-audit-2026-09-17.md) records the calculations, primary sources, data-access limits, retained findings and deferred work. Fertility has confirmed construction defects; no valid replacement causal estimate is claimed. Disability's second-minus-first contrast survives, but parity with whites does not follow. Neither short-run investment nor farm-owner losses identify aggregate innovation welfare. The political range lacks an identified lower endpoint and attribution denominator.

## Revisit if

Comparable family-history records identify descendant groups independently of current ethnicity; a corrected fixed-geography fertility design identifies the total effect; or direct evidence links a specified immigration intervention to net innovation, agglomeration or policy welfare. A publication or a larger sample alone does not clear these identification requirements.

## Supersedes

The specific interpretations in ladder entries 93–97, not their archived source material or all earlier fiscal/crime findings.
