# 2026-09-17: Bind frontier claims to measured outcomes and explicit counterfactuals

## Context

The user authorized execution of the six-workstream research frontier and asked for substantive epistemic improvements across domains. New source checks and re-estimation test several prior headlines rather than treating cleaner terminology as sufficient.

## Alternatives considered

1. Retain a single generational improvement/decline story. This is easy to communicate but fails to distinguish outcomes, selected identities, missing family history and uncertainty.
2. Withhold every useful description until causal identification is complete. This discards reproducible information without resolving the actual missing links.
3. Retain tested descriptions, revise failed or overextended claims, and state the additional evidence needed for causal and welfare conclusions. Adopted for this tranche.

## Decision

- Withdraw the fixed AFQT percentile-to-SD conversion. Retain published percentile coefficients separately from public self-ID/family-history estimates. Do not claim a universal fourth-generation reversal or crime parity.
- Retain the first-to-second disability contrast and adjusted trust gaps at their measured level. Neither establishes a mechanism, immutable trait, institutional damage or precise absence of convergence.
- Name the partial fiscal balance separately from reference gaps. Treat NYC spending/exposure as accounting; do not infer post-exit welfare, efficiency or incumbent displacement from lower shelter counts.
- Keep policy evidence distinct by intervention, outcome, sample and version. Record the raw-mirror Bracero wage reconstruction as degraded provenance; do not count nonmatching employment tables as replicated. H-2B source-table checks do not substitute for raw-data reproduction.
- Distinguish annual invention flow from cumulative invention. Neither an annual effect approaching zero nor an equal-year geometric-ratio calculation identifies cumulative catch-up.
- Use source-tagged family migration history with mixed/unknown cases preserved; keep self-identification separate. No genetic fraction is inferred from country of birth.

This changes specific interpretations, not the project's central question, constitution or causal tree. Culture and overall welfare retain explicit evaluative frames.

## Evidence

[Integrated findings](../research/immigration-frontier-execution-2026-09-17.md) and [execution record](../infra/immigration-fiscal/frontier_execution_2026_09_17/README.md): official NLS benchmark and independent scalar checks; exact source joins; three-year replicate-weight CPS; corrected GSS design; NYC source/budget reconciliation; fiscal component identities; primary policy tables, raw-mirror regressions and independent solver; bounded institutional/contact/environment source checks.

The strongest alternative remains real persistent disadvantage obscured by noisy public definitions. Demonstrated sensitivity limits the strength of the current verdict; it does not prove that the alternative is false. No model agreement is treated as independent empirical evidence.
