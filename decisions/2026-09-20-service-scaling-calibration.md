# 2026-09-20: Test scaling without assuming a universal fiscal exponent

## Context

The operator proposed testing Geoffrey West-style scaling as an explanation for
lower incremental service costs. The preceding annual-account integration used
CBO-informed category responses, producing an effective 70–75% ordinary-service
response. A numerical resemblance to biological or city scaling is not a derivation.

## Alternatives considered

1. Apply biological 3/4 to every service: rejected; wrong measured object and poor
   held-out cross-district prediction compared with proportional spending.
2. Apply urban network 5/6 everywhere: retained only as a theory-only illustration;
   physical network volume does not equal monetary government operations.
3. Multiply existing CBO responses by scaling: rejected without evidence of distinct
   mechanisms; likely overlap cannot be treated as additional savings.
4. Test category/size/horizon heterogeneity and preserve the existing conditional
   account until a transportable response is identified: selected.

## Decision and evidence

The [executed test](../research/immigration-service-scaling-test-2026-09-20.md) finds
school panel elasticities .735 unweighted/.836 pupil-weighted, versus cross-sectional
.945/1.004. Small/medium/large district cross-sectional slopes are .849/.986/1.028.
Weighted instructional spending responds .922 in the panel. Every full-panel
within-state interval for ten service functions includes proportional response.
These are descriptive estimates, with quality, prices and endogenous growth unresolved.

Keep the CBO-informed annual comparison unchanged. Add the empirical findings and
the exact finite-removal formula r=[1−(1−s)^b]/s to the overall analysis. At the
national illustrative share, 5/6 implies 84.21% response, not 70% or 20%. A uniform
application yields $214–232bn conditional annual cost, an explicitly unsupported
geographic/category transfer useful for showing what the theory would imply.

Prior position · New school/state data and primary urban-scaling evidence · Threshold
for a universal or causal coefficient not cleared · HOLD on fiscal calibration;
PARTIAL-UPDATE on descriptive support for sublinear school spending.

## Revisit if

Category-specific shocks, local service-user shares, regional prices and stable
quality establish transportable responses; or long-run follow-up shows catch-up.
Numerical proximity to 3/4 or 5/6 alone does not clear that threshold.

## Supersedes

No prior calculation is erased. Qualifies the mechanism behind
[category service responses](2026-09-20-category-service-response.md) and the
[complete annual account](2026-09-20-complete-account-and-fiscal-response.md).
