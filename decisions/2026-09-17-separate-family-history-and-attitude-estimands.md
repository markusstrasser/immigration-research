# 2026-09-17: Separate recovered family history from identity and outcome denominators

## Context

The user requested organization and substantive analysis of the September17 survey collection. Additional NLS parent-interview fields were already held, seven Pew surveys could be harmonized, and three lawful public LNS author replications supplied partial respondent data despite the account restriction on the original ICPSR release.

## Alternatives considered

1. Retain supplemental-only NLS categories: reproducible but discards main-interview parent history.
2. Treat roster position as identity or collapse all political responses into one assimilation scale: simpler, but contradicted by corrected IDs, source definitions and denominator arithmetic.
3. Recover source-tagged family history, preserve unknowns, and publish separate outcome estimands. More explicit and independently testable; selected small samples still limit interpretation.

## Decision

Adopt option3 for these analyses. Corrected YOUTH biological-parent IDs govern NLS identity, with responding-parent ID taken from the parent roster and explicit stored-ID joins. Keep older calculations as historical diagnostics. Use family migration history separately from national-origin self-identification; do not infer genomic fractions or unobserved Mexican lineage. Preserve legal-territory wording differences.

Pew party comparisons must name all-respondent, valid-category or major-party-only denominators. The 2015 paired nonidentifier calibration does not automatically transfer to other outcomes/years. LNS author derivatives are registered as transformed partial sources, never as the acquired original ICPSR data; no cross-package join without validated IDs. Policy response routes remain separate.

## Revisit if

Primary variable corrections or a verified country-specific lineage crosswalk change assignments; authorized raw LNS data explain the replication discrepancy; or design-based, ancestry-inclusive comparisons alter descriptive patterns. A stronger narrative alone is not new evidence.
