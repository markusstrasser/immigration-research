# 2026-09-23: Grade both sides by the same rules and price benefits as carefully as costs

## Context

The study integrity audit (93fe5af) found no fabricated studies among 26 sources. The repo
overstated studies on each side at the same 67% rate, and the two double standards it found both
favoured immigration: the Lott dismissal and the ethnicity proxy accepted in Freedman–Owens–Bohn.
It proposed four protocol rules. The same day the repo priced several social costs beside the
fiscal account (crime victims' harm, congestion, unreimbursed care, housing and wage transfers)
while the matching benefits were priced only in part. The operator approved the rules and asked
that benefits and gains be researched and calculated too.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Leave grading to judgment.** The audit shows judgment tilted in one direction in the two
   cases that mattered most.
2. **Adopt the four grading rules only.** It fixes how studies are graded but leaves the priced
   ledger lopsided: costs beside the account, benefits scattered or unpriced.
3. **Adopt the four rules plus a pricing-symmetry rule** (selected).

## Decision

The five rules in `notes/quant-bias-checklist.md` § "Evidence-symmetry rules" apply to every memo,
ladder entry and chat answer. Rule 5 opens a benefits program: scale and human-capital spillovers
and innovation (the counterpart of congestion), household services and native labour supply,
construction labour and housing supply, and labour-market mobility. Each is priced in its own
lane, then reported beside the costs with the same overlap rules.

- A later audit finds the rules applied unevenly, or finds the lean reversed.
- A benefit or cost channel cannot be priced to a common standard; then both sides of that channel
  are reported unpriced together.
