# 2026-10-15: The main case removes foreign-source corporate income, completes the union, recomputes the non-members who stay, and carries the Census undercount: $369.0–459.2bn

## Context

Round 1 of the critique loop on v7 (six packets, 347 findings; Gemini 4 Argon, GPT-6 Astra and Opus 5.5; the review
record `research/immigration-v7-assumption-review-2026-10-14.md`) found four corrections to the case over its $5bn bar
or its method test. Each was executed in its own lane and rerun by the parent. The operator (2026-10-14 16:18 JST):
"run /critique ... then fix the issues ... then do it again .. until it's mostly nitpicks or +- 5B$".

1. **Foreign-source income in item 6** (the A cluster). Item 6's corporate lines carried the US tax on foreign-source
   income, which does not come from capital at US jobs. IRS SOI's corporate foreign tax credit table (TY2021, column
   19, $59.280bn) measures it (`job_capital_key_refine_2026_10_14`, K2).
2. **The union's reporting rule** (B10, B24). v7's generation rule dropped US-born people who report Mexican origin and
   have one US-born parent (456,936, SE 48,569) and their observed children who do not report it (60,500)
   (`union_completion_2026_10_14`).
3. **The non-members who stay in mixed units** (the B cluster, about 45 findings). v7 recomputed only child credits
   (item 5b) and the added residual's taxes (D); the stayers' own returns, state taxes, refunds, benefits, health
   takeup, consumption and owner property stayed at their allocated values (`mixed_unit_removal_2026_10_14`).
4. **The Census undercount** (the coverage cluster). v7 kept the 2020 PES Hispanic net undercount (4.99%, SE 0.53
   points) beside as an upper bound that "the 2024 controls partly absorb". The Vintage 2024 controls keep the Census
   Hispanic total (−0.00787%), so the survey inherits the undercount; the blended base runs +8.8% on Hispanic children
   under 5 (`coverage_bound_2026_10_14`). The Mexico-born are covered too: the account sets their count at the ACS 2024
   level (audit row 4), and the ACS is controlled to the same Census-2020-based estimates, so v7's "Mexico-born count
   fixed" wording was wrong.

The same round tested item 6's low-end premise (`corporate_rents_2026_10_14`, 63862fb9): the payload's rule is mobile
capital whose owners earn the same after-tax return elsewhere, not "rents scale with the market" as v7's decision said.

The operator approved, on 2026-10-15 01:02 JST, the Census coverage and the mixed-unit removal in the central, and
item 6's low end kept at response 1 with the corrected premise wording.
