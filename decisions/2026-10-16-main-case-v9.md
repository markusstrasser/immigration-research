# 2026-10-16: The main case prices the capital charge and the promises at one rate, the Trustees', and charges no military service cost against a fixed defense: $344.0–412.6bn

## Context

Round 5 of the critique loop reviewed main case v8 ($365.8–456.0bn) as a whole and checked its frame (record:
`research/immigration-v7-assumption-review-2026-10-14.md`, "Round 5"). Sol verifiers checked every finding at source;
the parent spot-checked the over-bar ones. Two findings were over the $5bn bar, and five smaller ones were measured
in their own lanes:

1. **Two rates for one concept.** The account charged the return on public capital at 2% real at the low end and 3%
   at the high end. Its own decision called that rate "what the money costs" and took 3% from OMB A-4 (2003) as "the
   real rate of return on long-term government debt". The Social Security and Part A promises were discounted at the
   Trustees' path, about 2%, at both ends. So within the high end, one concept carried two rates. Operator, 2026-10-15
   11:47 JST: "The recommended rate fix values Social Security promises at 3%, above the roughly 2% that markets and
   the Trustees use." (`one_rate_2026_10_15`)
2. **Military service costs against a fixed defense.** Defense is held at zero response, so a fixed military means a
   fixed number of veterans. What military service earns is a cost of defense, whoever serves. v8 nevertheless charged
   the group's veterans' compensation, VA medical care, GI Bill, life insurance and TRICARE at response 1
   (`spending_keys_r5_2026_10_15`, item 13).
3. **Smaller measured items:**
   - income-security administration's retirement block (item 14);
   - public colleges' response by budget block (item 15);
   - native Mexican-origin minors calibrated to the ACS (item 16);
   - the missed people's tenure from PES Table D (item 17);
   - the food and SNAP sales-tax exemptions (item 18).

   A sixth, pass-through owners' taxes on the business capital at the group's jobs (item 19), was measured but is
   not adopted (alternative 6).

## Alternatives considered

1. **One rate per end, 2% at the low end and 3% at the high, for both legs** (arm A: $367.6–419.3bn on v8). Rejected.
   Neither leg has a market basis for 3% in 2024. The Trustees' path has a flat equivalent of 2.08%, and the 2024
   averages of the 10- and 30-year TIPS yields were 1.94% and 2.15% (FRED, below).
2. **Keep v8's two rates.** Rejected: one concept, two rates within one specification.
3. **Military service costs at response 1 (v8).** Rejected: it contradicts the fixed-defense premise. The
   means-tested veterans' pension keeps response 1, because it depends on the veteran's own income.
4. **Administration keyed by population (−$7.7bn on v8) or by the whole income-security line (the −$12.7bn screen).**
   Rejected: administration follows caseloads. The current key (16.4%) sits between SNAP's caseload share (16.2%) and
   Medicaid's (19.4%), so only the retirement block, keyed to Social Security caseloads, moves.
5. **Sales and customs taxes on business purchases keyed to production** (+$12.1bn on v8). Beside. It assigns
   input taxes to production, against the case's consumption incidence (CBO's rule), and the purchaser split is
   unmeasured.
6. **Item 6's key completed by legal form, as a pair: beside.**
   - Item 19, pass-through owners' taxes on the capital at the group's jobs: −$3.37 / −0.67bn at the national
     individual-owner proxy; −$5.02 / −1.19bn at the narrow full-FTC diagnostic; 0 under identical domestic
     replacement.
   - W09, the corporate lines re-keyed for compliance and legal form: +$3.30 / +1.22bn.

   One lane delivered both, with no identified central for either, as refinements of item 6's key in opposite
   directions. Adopting only the cost-lowering one would apply two standards (operator, 2026-10-15 13:14 JST: "Where
   v9's workers couldn't settle a number, the main agent adopted two cost-lowering items but kept a similar
   cost-raising one out."). Both are set aside together. Their naive sum is −$0.07 / +$0.55bn, inside the bar.
7. **The missed people's tenure with counts changed** (the Table D transfer): an arm. The central keeps item 12's
   counts.
