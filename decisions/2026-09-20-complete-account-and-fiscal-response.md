# 2026-09-20: Complete current accounting and fiscal response

## Context

The user authorized completing the annual cost/benefit calculation and asked what
could actually be established after administrative checks exposed major national
coverage gaps. The prior partial ledger mixed missing flows with allocation and
fiscal-basis differences. A national residual alone could not identify ethnic
incidence, but leaving it unresolved was not the only available result.

## Decision

Use the pinned2024 NIPA consolidated current account as the complete annual
accounting boundary. Allocate each disjoint receipt/expenditure category under
declared survey/program proxies; retain an evidence-only diagnostic with unknowns.
Separate incidence assignments from response coefficients before combining the
account with outside-resident production welfare. Keep tax/private counterparts,
business subsidies and ownership boundaries explicit. Export sign-reversal
thresholds for unknown effects rather than converting them into invented estimates.

## Alternatives considered

1. Continue the partial ledger: reproducible, but missing national flows and not
   adequate for a complete current-account claim.
2. Uniformly allocate every national residual to the group: simple but contradicted
   by the tax-tail, health-scope and government-grant checks.
3. Treat complete assigned balances as causal costs: rejected because fixed common
   costs, tax incidence, ownership and endogenous responses are different objects.
4. Stop because historical absence is unobserved: rejected; a labeled structural
   estimate and break-even analysis are feasible without causal overstatement.

## Evidence

The [calculation record](../research/immigration-complete-annual-account-2026-09-20.md)
links source partitions, assumptions, tests and executed results. Both tax and
spending totals close exactly; the benefit bridge preserves private/tax transfers.
The subsidy review changed the response contract before the joined result: business
subsidies are held fixed unless a matching included-owner receipt is modeled.
The primary long-run grid stays negative, while the fixed-service path can be
positive. This is a conditional sign result, not a causal empirical bound.

## Revisit if

Origin-linked administrative tax/benefit data, program-specific outside-CPS costs,
identified service-cost responses, current capital/ownership parameters, or a
matched estimate of omitted net effects becomes available. A prospective admission
or lifetime question requires a different population and dynamic counterfactual.

## Supersedes

The prior partial annual ledger as the preferred complete current-account view.
Its data and calculations remain intact. No older lifetime, crime or historical
counterfactual result is mechanically validated or updated by this decision.
