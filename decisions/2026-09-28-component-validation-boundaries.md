# 2026-09-28: Keep validation claims tied to the quantity actually tested

## Context

The operator authorized the four follow-ups in the validation memo: school
service adjustment, the elderly medical discrepancy, historical fiscal component
prediction and a coherent joint Mariel school budget. All four now have executed
results, declared comparisons and retained unfavorable alternatives. [EXECUTION]

## Alternatives considered

1. Treat selected predictive successes as validation of the complete national
   counterfactual. Rejected: the tests do not observe that counterfactual, several
   use later totals as inputs, and several fail their naive comparison. [INFERENCE]
2. Treat failed component tests as proving no immigration costs. Rejected: the
   failed objects are specific transport rules and measurement constructions,
   while actual resource use and other corroborated inputs remain. [INFERENCE]
3. Retain component-specific evidence, correct a disproven population-equivalence
   claim, and leave national parameters unchanged by these tests. Adopted below.

## Decision

- Record useful but mixed temporal fiscal and school prediction; retain weighting,
  outcome and policy-regime failures. Do not turn within-CPS prediction into an
  independent administrative tax check. [CALCULATION / INFERENCE]
- Reject the new shared-weight Mariel model as a validated joint counterfactual:
  both fixed specifications lose the pretreatment prediction comparison. This is
  a decision about that construction, not all Mariel literature. [CALCULATION]
- Correct the earlier claim that common institutional exclusions dismiss that
  explanation for MEPS–MCBS disagreement. The exclusions differ; the remaining
  gap's cause is unresolved. An imprecise smaller 2022 gap is not equivalence.
  [SOURCE / CALCULATION]
- No coefficient, ethnicity multiplier or national-total change follows
  mechanically. Final calibrated allocation keys and the complete response model
  still need independent validation of their own. [INFERENCE]

## Revisit if

A policy-updated rule predicts reserved administrative outcomes; a joint school
counterfactual passes declared prediction checks and a credible causal design;
or medical sources can be compared on identical enrollment, service-payment and
population definitions. Predictive fit would still not settle causal identification
or whose welfare counts. [INFERENCE]

sentence is corrected and its proposed follow-ups are replaced by executed results.
