# 2026-09-20: Measure pupil exposure and preserve residual boundaries

## Context

The user authorized execution of the four checks recommended in the September19
gap diagnosis. The uniform public-enrollment factor and national accounting
residuals were material uncertainties in the annual fiscal account.

## Alternatives considered

1. Retain the uniform 80.27% school factor despite actual public-school data.
2. Transport October2024 measured age/origin enrollment rates into the existing
   ASEC population and cost schedules, with sparse cells pooled and alternatives
   retained.
3. Force the full model to official national totals using proportional origin
   adjustments before resolving differences in population, accounting and tax units.

## Decision

Use measured enrollment as the current annual version. Retain ages5–17-only,
ages3–24 and pooled-origin alternatives; label survey-month and cost transport.
Preserve older age-profile lifetime outputs as dated, unrevised scenarios.

Do not allocate national receipt or medical-spending residuals proportionally.
IRS/CPS tax discrepancies vary sharply across income bands, while Medicaid
comparisons still mix payer, service, residence and coverage concepts. The seven
zeroed federal-function gross pools also cannot close the spending gap until
their grant overlap with final state/local spending is resolved.

## Evidence

The [executed memo](../research/immigration-four-fiscal-checks-2026-09-20.md) links
source locks, generators, replicate calculations and held-out comparisons.
Measured school exposure changes the target annual balance by −$25.040bn shared
/−$26.937bn personal. Public source-year checks also correct the earlier IRS
anchors from2023 to2022. Independent code review caught and removed unconsolidated
grants from the national spending bridge and prior-year healthcare exposure for
survey-year newborns.

## Revisit if

Matched administrative pupil exposure overturns the transported school rates;
origin-specific administrative tax/medical data establish a correction; or a
complete ownership crosswalk resolves the national residual. Aggregate matching
alone does not establish any of those conditions.

## Supersedes

The uniform enrollment construction for the current annual release only.
The September19 finance and lifetime source versions remain reproducible evidence.
