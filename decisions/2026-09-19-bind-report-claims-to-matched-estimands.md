# Bind current conclusions to the calculations that support them

The operator requested a material cross-check of the last five days' results,
frameworks and assumptions, then specified an index of evidence rather than
generated narrative. The [result index](../research/immigration-five-day-cross-check-2026-09-19.md)
records verified defects, surviving descriptions and unfinished calculations.

Retain the descriptive earnings, modeled tax and custody comparisons within
their actual populations. Withdraw the actionable birth-versus-arrival subsidy
budget: the existing alternative allocation changes the key contrast's sign.
Mark expanded absolute balances and their payer/interest derivatives provisional
until intergovernmental transfers and omitted receipt categories are reconciled.

Withdraw hard-refutation grades based on unmatched populations, time horizons
or welfare outcomes. Failed IV identification does not establish a causal null.
Stock-to-flow crime prices, victim-age proxies and attitude-based model parameters
remain scenarios or unvalidated bridges unless their specific identification
conditions are met. Preserve contradictory evidence and uncertainty in both
political directions.

The alternative of deleting the empirical work is rejected: core descriptions
survive and selected arithmetic independently reproduces. The alternative of
retaining stronger claims because their input numbers are real is rejected:
the pronatal reversal, grant overlap and scalar-invariant IV expose material
failures at the interpretation and accounting layers.

Existing evidence is retained with dated corrections. This decision changes
claim confidence and interpretation, not the constitution, central question or
causal protocol. Reopen each downgraded claim when its specific missing
calculation in the result index is completed.
