# Preserve measured categories and distinguish missingness mechanisms

The operator authorized completing the fiscal, admission and conduct work, then requested explicit modeling of missing unauthorized residents, selective enforcement, Hispanic/White coding and Black wage/crime groups. The current [synthesis](../research/immigration-clarity-update-2026-09-05.md) records the resulting evidence. The mission and causal question remain unchanged.

Five different mechanisms were considered before choosing the analyses: survey undercoverage; latent immigration status; outcome underreporting/selection; classification error; and omitted or overlapping fiscal components. A universal multiplier cannot represent them: some errors move a rate's numerator and denominator in opposite directions.

Use source-native categories, then explicit crosswalks that retain unresolved dimensions. The Light baseline is already native-born and its legal-immigrant group includes naturalized citizens. Its previous double subtraction is removed. SPI uses RV0004; citizenship alone cannot imply nativity. Rates require a source-labeled matched population year/universe, otherwise the explicit counts-only product withdraws invalid rates. New race strata preserve Hispanic ethnicity, multiracial overlap and measured birthplace separately.

Expand fiscal accounting through official CPS tax-model fields and once-only resource-unit allocation. Keep actual tax collection distinct from model liabilities/refunds. Transport MEPS health expenditure with stated donor definitions and propagated covariance. Use actual ACS public-pupil exposure for a separately labeled school scenario; remove SPM lunch before combining total school spending that already includes food. The CPS resource-unit/ACS household and timing mismatch remains explicit, not a common-person ledger.

Use algebraic tipping points and both-sided coverage/selection stress rather than a fabricated prior for unknown unknowns. Preserve documented Casey/Minnesota institutional failures without treating them as an estimated U.S. status detection ratio. Do not infer a causal race effect from descriptive wage or imprisonment differences, nor current-cohort conduct from older aggregate status data.

Alternatives rejected: adding the whole unauthorized stock to Census participants (double-counts included residents); mapping all foreign-born to noncitizens (misclassification); replacing native with White without renaming the estimand (population switch); retaining mixed-year prison rates with a footnote (does not measure either year); using school-age children as enrolled pupils or combining overlapping lunch expenditure (wrong exposure/ledger); treating source disagreement as independent measurements to average (shared inputs and different vintages).

The staged full warehouses preserve unrelated contents and the existing fiscal tensor. New descriptive analyses remain reproducible separate products. Revisit these conclusions when matched status/race/disposition records, administrative tax-benefit linkage, audited marginal service costs, or independent evidence of detection/coverage becomes available. A narrower sampling interval alone does not resolve those gaps.
