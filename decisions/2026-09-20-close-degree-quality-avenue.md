# 2026-09-20: Close the current degree-quality avenue after PIAAC

## Context

The user challenged credential attainment as a measure of comparable education, asked whether better datasets existed, and authorized one final adult-skills comparison before stopping at diminishing returns. NLSY97 yielded an adolescent ASVAB gap among selected later BA holders; the university-tier audit identified public origin suppression and selected degree-path measures.

## Decision and evidence

Stop additional modeling on these public data after the executed PIAAC check. The official2012/14 and separate2017 releases contain only32 and28 eligible Hispanic BA+ holders. Both literacy and numeracy point estimates favor the non-Hispanic-white group, but all main approximate95% intervals include zero. BA-only and age/sex sensitivity estimates remain inconclusive. National mean/SE benchmarks reproduce at published precision. [SOURCE: [PIAAC results and methods](../infra/immigration-fiscal/education_quality_2026_09_20/PIAAC_RESULT.md)]

Retain the NLSY result within its observed cohort, identity and adolescent-score scope. Do not turn PIAAC nonsignificance into equality, merge its score points with NLSY percentiles, count correlated specifications as independent confirmations, or translate either study into a causal school-quality or fiscal estimate. [SOURCE: [NLSY and dataset audit](../infra/immigration-fiscal/education_quality_2026_09_20/RESULT.md); INFERENCE]

## Alternatives considered

Further age/sex/generation slices would shrink already small samples. Informal cross-release pooling needs assumptions about targets and survey covariance and would not restore Mexican ancestry. Public ELS degree-tier analysis concerns a broader Hispanic group and a selected path that excludes known associate-to-BA transfers; its origin fields are suppressed. None settles the original exact-ancestry university-quality question. [SOURCE: executed field audits; INFERENCE]

## Revisit if

A materially larger sample jointly observes ancestry independently of current ethnic identity, degree-awarding institutions and/or comparable skills; or a relevant, adequately powered primary analysis becomes available. Restricted access without adequate relevant sample size is not sufficient by itself. [INFERENCE]

## Supersedes

Closes the previously proposed final PIAAC calculation. Extends the [ancestry-outcome data ceiling](2026-09-20-ancestry-outcome-data-ceiling.md) to this follow-up; does not retract the earlier observed statistics or change fiscal totals.
