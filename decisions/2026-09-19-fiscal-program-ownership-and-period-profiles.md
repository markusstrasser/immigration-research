# 2026-09-19: Count fiscal programs once and propagate their actual ages

The user requested repair of yearly and lifetime costs after the September 19 audit. The former expansion duplicated some federal grants, veterans cash and enforcement; its downstream lifetime model spread each group's average adjustment equally over all ages.

Use explicit program ownership and source hashes. Retain the partial benchmark, net only mapped service fees/grants, and label the expansion's unresolved coverage. Keep both household-sharing and source/recipient attribution; use the latter for period-profile NPVs. Export components at each age band, apply conditional NVSS person-years, and report real discount/public-goods sensitivities. Preserve historical evidence and block stale consumers.

Rejected: subtracting every federal grant indiscriminately (education/Medicaid already netted and transit absent from G); crediting every fee (many concern excluded services); the $44.499bn broad housing correction (direct renter aid need not belong to G); calling period profiles birth/admission forecasts; adding whole-career SSA money's-worth ratios as annual entitlement accrual.

Evidence: Census/OMB source rows and mechanically checked identities, the actual CPS weighted veterans component, the personal/shared profile comparison, conditional-survival identity tests, and the integrated code review. See the [current calculation index](../research/immigration-yearly-lifetime-cost-repair-2026-09-19.md) for primary sources, figures, coverage limits and reproduction.

Revisit when recipient-matched federal/state accounts, current program coverage ratios, longitudinal entry/exit/earnings cohorts, or an independently validated pension accrual model become available. Those sources could support a more complete account or genuine cohort forecasts; arithmetic checks alone cannot.
